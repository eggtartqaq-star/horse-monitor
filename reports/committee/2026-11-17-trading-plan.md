## 1. Market atmosphere in one paragraph

**A late-cycle, inflationary expansion with the Fed raising rates — not a recession.** GDP +3.4%,
unemployment 4.1%, PPI +6.5% y/y; the Fed hiked in September and a December hike is ~80% priced;
the 10-year is ~5.13–5.16% (13 Nov) and the dollar is at its 2026 high. That is why gold and long
bonds are falling together. On top of it, AI and semiconductors are unwinding: SMH −31% from its
June high, NVDA −36% with a CEO resignation. Money has rotated into staples and healthcare at a
record pace (best XLP month since 1998), while the average stock (equal-weight S&P) is only 2.4%
off its high. **It is a rotation out of the leaders, not a broad bear market.** NVDA's beat-and-raise
tonight (+4% after hours) is, until it holds for several sessions, a relief bounce.

the biggest inflow since 2001 is the same mistake as chasing memory in June, in a quieter form.
