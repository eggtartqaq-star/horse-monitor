**Why it exists:** a Korea Securities Depository rule caps ADR creation. The cap was hit
within two weeks of listing. Nobody can create new ADS to sell against the premium, so
arbitrage cannot close it. **The premium is a regulatory artefact, not a view on the business.**

**What it means for a holder at $129:**
- The company is repurchasing its own ordinary shares at roughly **$98 per ADS-equivalent**.
  The Owner paid $129 for the same economic claim.
- If the KSD or FSC **lift or widen the cap**, the ADS can fall 30%+ toward parity **with no
  change in SK hynix's business**. There is no date on this. It is the single largest
  structural risk on the instrument and it sits entirely outside the memory cycle.
- Conversely, the premium is why the ADS fell **less** than the ordinary in the October
  selloff (−20% vs −31%). It is a cushion on the way down and an overhang on the way up.
- **Reduce trigger, level-independent (老詹):** any sharp narrowing of the premium while
  Seoul is flat or up means the conversion valve has opened. Sell the ADS leg on that
  signal regardless of chart level.

## The business — excellent statements measuring a cycle peak

**巴爺爺: B+ / 72.** The statements alone would be A− (~88). The −16 is cycle position.
*Roughly 60% of what makes them look excellent is price, not structure.*

- 3Q26: revenue ₩104.6tn, operating profit ₩83.3tn, **80% operating margin**, ₩98.7tn net
  cash. Nine-month OP is 3.8x all of 2025.
- **The number that decides it: 13% and <0%.** That is the share of peak operating profit
  that survived the last two troughs (2018→19; 2021→23). Peak to trough took 4–5 quarters
  both times. A holder at $129 is underwriting that this time the floor is higher.
- **The HBM thesis is currently inverted.** The 80% margin is being driven by commodity
  DRAM pricing (ASP +high-20s% q/q on ~10% bit growth), and TrendForce estimates HBM
  per-wafer profitability fell *below* DDR5 RDIMM in 1Q26. **This contradicts the "HBM
  margins 2–3x DDR5" line in the firm's own 21 Aug MSFT industry report, which is now
  stale and should not be reused.**
- **The depreciation trap:** D&A is ~4% of revenue today only because revenue is inflated.
  Capex stepping to ₩50–80tn/yr means D&A of ₩30–40tn/yr by 2029 — 50%+ of a trough-year
  revenue. This is the mechanism by which 80% margins become losses.
- Net income is unusable in 2026 (~₩96tn non-operating swing across two quarters). Use OP
  and cash.

## The cycle — NEUTRAL, downgraded

**Elon Musk: Memory NEUTRAL, 6/10, down from August's implicit overweight. Hold with
tripwires; not a place to add.**

- Conventional DRAM contract: peak rate of increase was 1Q26 (+93–98%); three consecutive
  quarters of deceleration since (+58–63 → +13–18 → +10–15% forecast). **NAND went flat in
  October after 21 straight months up.**
- **Eight of twelve cycle-turn indicators are flashing or partial** — the configuration
  seen two to four quarters *before* a peak, not after one. Flashing: all three majors
  raising capex in one window; NAND and consumer DRAM momentum rolling; hyperscaler
  raise-cadence stopped; second-source HBM qualification (Samsung at Nvidia, Feb 2026);
  LTA price *ceilings*; SK hynix losing 4pp DRAM share in its 80%-margin quarter; stocks
  selling off on record prints. **Not flashing:** spot below contract; realised bit
  oversupply (scheduled 2H27–28).
- HBM is volume-locked through 2029 across 14 LTAs, but **priced annually with ceilings,
  not floors** — locked in the customer's favour, not the Owner's. Nvidia now has three
  qualified sources; Counterpoint expects Samsung to lead HBM in 2027.
- **SKHY is the expensive way to own the cycle on a risk-adjusted basis** — most margin and
  most Nvidia share to lose, heaviest crowding, plus a 45–55% premium the ordinary does not
  carry. The cheap way only if HBM pricing holds through 2028.

## Regulatory — one dated risk, one action item

- **Wuxi DRAM fab (~one-third of DRAM wafer output) runs on a one-year US tool licence
  that expires 31 December 2026 — seven weeks out.** No 2027 licence has been reported
  issued. Frozen at the 1a node; a cash machine in a shortage, a stranded asset in a
  downturn. The Dalian NAND licence is openly under negotiation and tied to a US fab
  commitment.
- No Section 232 tariff on Korean memory today. Lutnick's "100% on memory not made in
  America" is a stated intent, not a rule.
- **Owner action:** Korean dividend withholding is **22% at source** unless treaty
  certification is on file through the depositary, which drops it to **15%**. Confirm with
  the broker before the next record date. Korean capital-gains tax does not reach a Nasdaq
  sale of the ADS.

   rising) points higher — cannot say the peak is reached. Elon Musk: eight of twelve

   turn before the quarter arrives, which is what 29 Oct–6 Nov looked like.
