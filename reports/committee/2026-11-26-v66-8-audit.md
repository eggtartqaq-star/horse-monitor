**H-1 · Paper fills: the instructions contradict each other, and the checklist claims code behaviour that does not exist.**
- **The contradiction:**
  - G4 (:255) tells the Owner to record paper fills with `fill open` and says 「`switch_date` 未填 = 全部當模擬」.
  - F :214 and README :144 say 「模擬交易唔好用 fill 記錄(fills.csv 只記真錢交易)」.
  - F :207 says 「V6.6 只跑掃描做記錄」. README :3 still points paper trading at V6.4.2.
- **The code:** `switch_date` is used only by the `--legacy` gate (`ledger_v66.py:271`). Nothing marks fills as paper. At the switch, paper rows would:

  - enter the S1–S3 review alongside real trades.

- **The trade-off:** without paper fills, Telegram ④ (daily stops) and the E1 10-day note cannot cover paper positions. That is a reason for the CRO to rule quickly, not a reason to bypass the ruling.
