# KLA Corporation (KLAC) — Management Quality Review
**Analyst:** Peter (Dept. 2, Equity Research) · **Reference date:** 2026-07-19 · **Management grade: A−**

Best-in-class execution and capital-allocation discipline, a value-creating M&A record, and a clean integrity screen. The minus reflects (1) succession opacity around a 65-year-old, 20-year-tenure CEO with no publicly named successor, and (2) an insider-transaction record that is entirely sell-side (all 10b5-1/planned).

## Leadership snapshot
- **Rick Wallace** — President & CEO since January 2006; age 65. Still in the seat mid-2026; **no succession announced** despite a 20-year tenure.
- **Bren Higgins** — CFO; long-tenured and stable (integrity positive).
- **Ahmad Khan** — President, Semiconductor Products & Customers; most-cited internal successor candidate. No COO.
- **Robert Calderoni** — independent Board Chair since Nov 2022; **Kevin Kennedy** chairs Audit (since Nov 2024). Independent-chair structure is a governance positive.

## Promise-vs-delivery record (recent quarters)
- **Q3 FY2026 (qtr ended 3/31/26):** Revenue **$3.415B vs guided midpoint $3.35B** (beat). Non-GAAP EPS **$9.40**, GAAP **$9.12**, both above midpoints. Gross margin **62.2%, ~45bps above midpoint**.
- **Q4/June-qtr guidance:** revenue **$3.575B ±$200M**; non-GAAP EPS **$9.87 ±$1** (pre-split).
- **Cycle framing:** raised 2026 WFE outlook to **$140B+**; guided advanced-packaging revenue to nearly double to **~$1B in 2026**.
- **Candor on headwinds:** CFO held a CY2026 gross-margin target of ~62% ±50bps while explicitly flagging memory-pricing headwinds and tariff impacts (~100bps negative over several quarters) — guiding down margin honestly is a favorable integrity signal.
- **Longer-term target met:** the prior analyst-day "~$38 EPS by 2026" goal is effectively achieved (annualized non-GAAP run-rate ~$38).

## Capital allocation scorecard — strong
- **Dividend:** 17th consecutive annual increase; quarterly raised to $2.30 pre-split in March 2026, +21% from $1.90; post-split $0.23/qtr from Aug 2026. Uninterrupted growth through two WFE downturns.
- **Buybacks:** $7B authorization (2026) atop a $5B program (April 2025, $3.94B remaining at 12/31/25); $797M returned in Q2 FY26 alone. Steady, sometimes counter-cyclical repurchase that has materially shrunk the share count.
- **Stock split:** 10-for-1, effective 6/12/26 — cosmetic/liquidity, neutral to allocation.

## M&A record — net value-creating
- **Orbotech (closed 2/20/2019):** ~$3.4B equity / $3.2B EV, $65.93/share; seeded the advanced-packaging franchise now approaching ~$1B — value-accretive.
- **Abandoned Lam-KLA merger:** agreed 2015, terminated 10/5/2016 after DOJ declined a consent decree; no termination fees. Remaining independent was clearly the better outcome — disciplined.

## Insider activity — routine selling only, no buys
- **CEO Rick Wallace:** sold 4,512 shares 6/11/26 at avg $2,213.37 (pre-split); ~$21M across two planned transactions (Nov 2025, May 2026) under a 10b5-1 plan.
- **CAO Kirloskar:** 297 shares 5/11/26 at $1,879.02.
- **Net insider selling ~-$15.9M over trailing 90 days**; all appear planned/10b5-1; no open-market purchases, no unusual cluster. Signal: neutral-to-mildly negative but unremarkable.

## Governance flags
1. **Succession opacity (primary flag):** 65-year-old CEO, ~20-year tenure, no publicly named successor; internal candidate (Khan) exists but no timeline. Key-person/transition risk.
2. All insider activity is sell-side — routine, worth monitoring.
3. Clean elsewhere: no restatements, no abrupt CFO departure, independent board chair, no related-party or guidance-gaming red flags.

## What this team would do in a downturn
Protect R&D and margin, keep raising the dividend, lean into buybacks (sustained both through the 2019 and 2023 WFE troughs; sticky service revenue cushions the systems business). The 2016 Lam walk-away shows willingness to forgo a headline deal on discipline.
