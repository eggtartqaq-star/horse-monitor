**Shape of the cycle.** Trough 1Q23 (OP –3.4tn) → breakeven 4Q23 → 2024 recovery (23.5tn, 35%) → 2025 (47.2tn, 49%) → 2026 vertical: nine-month operating profit of ~181.5tn is **3.8x the whole of 2025 and 8.7x the 2018 peak year**. 3Q26 alone (83.3tn) is **4x the 2018 full-year record**. There is no precedent in this company's history — or in the memory industry's — for an 80% operating margin. This is the single most important fact in the report: whatever one believes about HBM structural change, the current margin is at a level that has never been sustained by any memory maker.

**Note on consensus pattern:** 1Q26 and 2Q26 each came in slightly *below* consensus (2Q26 by ~5%, stock –9.6% on the day); 3Q26 beat by ~8–10%. ## 8. Peak-to-trough earnings — what survives

Company's own history, annual operating profit, KRW tn (sources in §1):

| Cycle | Peak year OP | Trough year OP | Trough / peak | Peak margin → trough margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2018 → 2019 | 20.84 | 2.7 | **13%** (–87%) | 52% → 10% |
| 2022 → 2023 | 6.81 (2022; the true peak was 2021 at ~12.4tn, **unverified** this session) | –7.73 | **negative** (below zero) | — → –24% |
| 2021 → 2023 (if 2021 peak ≈12.4tn is accepted) | ~12.4 (**unverified**) | –7.73 | **negative** | — |

**Reading:** in the milder cycle (2018–19) 13% of peak operating profit survived; in the harsher one (2022–23) *none* did — the company lost 7.7tn and 9.1tn at the net line. Quarterly amplitude is worse: 3Q18 to 4Q19 and 2Q22 to 1Q23 both went from record to loss within 4–5 quarters.

Applied to today (illustrative, not a forecast): if 3Q26 annualised OP (~333tn) were the peak, the 2018–19 template implies a trough-year OP of ~40–45tn (still above 2024's 23.5tn, roughly 2025's 47tn); the 2022–23 template implies a loss. The spread between those two outcomes — "still earning more than 2024" vs "losing money" — is the whole valuation debate, and the historical record does not let me rule out the second.
