## 1. Summary and grade

### Grade: **A− · 81/100** (August: A− 82; −1 for a dilution correction, see §6)

Q3 2026 was the best quarter in the company's history on every line:

- Revenue **$1,889.8M, +33.8%**, accelerating from +30% in Q2 [B1].
- GAAP operating income **$418.7M**, a **22.2% margin** [C], up from 11.5% a year earlier.
- GAAP net income **$384.7M** [C from B1] and FCF **$473.5M** [B1].
- FY2026 guidance raised again, to revenue of **$6.9–7.0B** and GAAP operating income of **$1.2–1.3B** [B1].

The open question I flagged in August was why guidance implied H2 operating income below H1. Q3 answered
it: **guidance was conservative.** Q3 alone delivered $418.7M against an implied H2 range of $425–525M [Aug].

**What holds the grade at A− rather than lifting it:**
1. **Guidance again implies a step-down.** It implies Q4 revenue of **$1.79–1.89B**, flat to down on Q3,
   and Q4 GAAP operating income of **$206–306M**, an 11.5–16.2% margin [C]. Q3 also contained about
   **$50M of US BRUKINSA order-timing benefit** that may not recur. That figure comes from a secondary
   source relaying management's comment [B3]. One review says management guided to **only modest margin
   expansion in 2027** [B3, secondary].
2. **Correction of my own August finding on adjusted earnings.** I now have stock comp:
   **SBC was $260.8M in H1 2026** [B5], about **76% of the H1 GAAP-to-adjusted operating income gap**
   [C]. In August I suspected the addback was mostly amortisation. **That was wrong. "Adjusted" mainly
   excludes stock comp, which is a real cost.** Use GAAP.
3. **Correction on dilution.** Diluted ADSs derived from net income and EPS rose about **+3.4% year on
   year** on a nine-month basis and about **+1.5% from Q2 to Q3** [C from B1]. In August I scored
   share-count creep as absent. That was too generous.
4. **A large one-off GAAP tax benefit is pending.** Management says 2026 earnings may support reversing
   valuation allowances, "resulting in a material tax benefit when recognized" [B1, B14]. One extraction
   puts the deferred tax assets under allowance at about $3.6B [B7, unconfirmed]. **When it lands, GAAP
   EPS will be inflated by a non-cash, non-recurring amount. The valuation analyst must strip it out.**
5. **Concentration is still rising.** BRUKINSA was **73.6%** of Q3 group revenue, and BRUKINSA US alone
   was **53.2%** [C]. In August the figures were about 71% and 52.5%.

### The 3 numbers that matter most

1. **GAAP operating margin of 22.2% in Q3 2026 (17.8% TTM)** [C]. Guidance implies 11.5–16.2% for Q4.
   Whether Q4 lands near Q3 or near the guide shows whether the guide is still conservative or opex is
   finally stepping up.
2. **BRUKINSA US sales of $1,005.1M, +36.1%** [B2]. That is 53.2% of group revenue and includes about
   $50M of timing [B3]. Excluding the timing benefit, growth was about **+29%** [E].
3. **TTM FCF of $1,449M, a 21.9% margin, 1.58x GAAP net income** [C]. Netting H1 SBC out of H1 FCF
   leaves an **FCF-less-SBC margin of about 10%** [C]. Cash generation is real, but the headline figure
   overstates owner economics by about $0.5B a year [E].

## 4. Profitability

### 4.1 The path to sustained GAAP profitability: it is already sustained

| Quarter | Q1'25 | Q2'25 | Q3'25 | Q4'25 | Q1'26 | Q2'26 | Q3'26 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GAAP net income ($M) | 1.27 [Aug] | ~94.2 [C] | ~125 [B1] | 66.5 [B6] | 227.4 [Aug] | 237.0 [Aug] | **384.7** [C] |
| GAAP op. income ($M) | ~11.0 [C] | 88 [Aug] | 163.1 [B2] | 185.0 [B6] | 249.9 | 325.0 | **418.7** |
| GAAP op. margin | ~1% [E] | ~6.7% [E] | 11.5% | 12.4% | 16.5% | 19.0% | **22.2%** |

Reconciliation [C]: Q1'25 + Q2'25 + Q3'25 + Q4'25 net income = 1.27 + 94.2 + 125 + 66.5 = $287.0M, which
matches reported FY2025 net income of $286.9M [B6]. Operating income: 11.0 + 88 + 163.1 + 185.0 =
$447.1M, which matches the reported $447.1M [B6]. The Q1'25 and Q2'25 revenue split behind the margins
is estimated, hence [E].

**Seven consecutive GAAP-profitable quarters, and operating margin has expanded every quarter.** The
question is no longer whether GAAP profitability is sustainable. It is how fast margins compound from
here, and on that the evidence is mixed:

- **For continued expansion:** SG&A leverage is still working. Q3 GAAP opex grew +19% against revenue
  +34% [B1]. Back-solving from those growth rates, SG&A was about 37% of revenue in Q3'25 and about 31%
  in Q3'26 [C, approximate]. Gross margin rose 2 points to 88% [B1].
- **Against:** the FY2026 guide implies Q4 GAAP operating income of $206–306M [C], an 11.5–16.2% margin.
  A third-party review says management guided to "only modest margin expansion next year" [B3,
  secondary, wording unverified]. Another review says late-stage trials and launches could add about
  $140M of quarterly expense [B13, low confidence]. R&D grew +26% in Q3 [B1], faster than in H1.

**Reading the guide:** this is the fourth time the shape has looked like this. Guidance was raised at
every 2026 print: Feb $6.2–6.4B [B6] → May $6.3–6.5B [Aug] → Aug $6.6–6.8B [Aug] → Nov $6.9–7.0B [B1].
On GAAP operating income it went $750–850M (May) → $1.0–1.1B (Aug) → **$1.2–1.3B (Nov)**. The track
record says the guide is conservative. But Q4 carries a known headwind (reversal of the timing benefit)
and a known cost step (R&D +26%). **My base case is that Q4 GAAP operating margin lands above the guided
11.5–16.2% but below Q3's 22.2%** [E].
