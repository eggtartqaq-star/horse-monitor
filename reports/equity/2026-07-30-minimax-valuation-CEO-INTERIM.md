## 2. The break-even that actually decides it

Discounting today's EV forward five years at **20%** — top-of-range, which is what a
no-moat, pre-profit, VIE/WVR-structured PRC issuer with negative ROIC warrants, and what
菲比斯 instructed — the midpoint EV of US$5.86bn must become **US$14.6bn** by mid-2031 just
to earn that rate of return. What revenue that requires depends entirely on the exit
multiple:

| Exit EV/Sales | Required 2031 revenue | vs the US$79m FY2025 base |
|---|---|---|
| 1.0x | US$14.6bn | 184x |
| 1.5x | US$9.7bn | 123x |
| 2.0x | US$7.3bn | 92x |
| **2.5x** | **US$5.8bn** | **74x** |
| 3.0x | US$4.9bn | 61x |
| 5.0x | US$2.9bn | 37x |

Consensus has revenue at ~US$5.8–6.0bn by 2030 — a year earlier than the row above, so the
comparison is generous to the bull case.

**The finding: at today's price the market is paying the FULL consensus ramp, and it only
works at an exit multiple of ~2.5x sales or better.** There is no margin of safety embedded
at consensus. You are not being paid to take the execution risk; you are paying for it in
advance.

**And 2.5x sales is a software multiple.** It requires a terminal gross margin that this
company does not have (25.4%) and that the two analysts who studied it both expect to move
the wrong way:

- 巴爺爺 cannot verify the 13.2pp gross-margin expansion is real engineering rather than
  favourable pricing from Alibaba, which is simultaneously a 13.66% shareholder and the
  supplier whose 9M2025 bill (US$58.4m) **exceeded total company revenue** (US$53.4m).
- 菲比斯 models the API segment — the only 69% gross-margin part of the business — compressing
  to 30–40% by 2028, and notes MiniMax ran the pricing-power test in public on 1 June 2026
  and opened a refund portal on 2 June.

**So the entire valuation rests on a terminal gross margin that one analyst cannot verify
and the other expects to fall.** That is the case in one sentence.

## 3. The bull case, stated honestly (anti-anchoring, L-003)

Five consecutive negative verdicts is precisely the condition under which this firm would
miss a real dislocation, so the bull case is stated at full strength:

- **On consensus 2030 revenue, EV/2030 sales is 0.87x–1.15x.** If you believe the ramp, the
  stock is not expensive at all — it is cheap. The 20% discount rate is doing the work in the
  table above, and a 12% rate would make today's price look like a bargain.
- The drawdown has a **mechanical** explanation, not only a fundamental one: a 9 July lock-up
  expiry freed 153m shares (48.9% of outstanding) against a prior free float of ~5%. Bear
  views were literally unexpressable before that date. Forced supply is the classic source of
  genuine dislocation.
- **Growth got cheaper:** revenue +159% while selling & distribution spend fell 40.3%.
- Insider behaviour is aligned, not fleeing: >80% of pre-IPO/cornerstone holders pledged
  long-term holdings on 27 July, the founding team voluntarily extended its lock-up by 12
  months, and the founder is forgoing salary and gifting 4% of equity to employees.
  (Aggregator-sourced; unconfirmed against filings.)
- A **Stock Connect decision lands ~6 August** — seven days away — with Bloomberg Intelligence
  sizing potential southbound inflow at up to HK$47bn.

**What would make me a buyer, stated in advance:** verified evidence that gross margin is
structurally rising toward 40%+ *with* the Alibaba compute cost disclosed separately, plus
stable or growing consumer MAU. That is a first-half-2026 interim question, due by 31 August.
**I found none of it today.** The judgement is unresolved on evidence, not decided by the
house's streak.

## 5. The HK$770 consensus target

3.6x spot, from 14 Buy / 1 Sell. Working backwards, a HK$770 target on the higher share
count is roughly a US$34bn market cap — which at a 3x exit multiple needs ~US$11bn of
revenue, roughly **double** the same houses' own 2030 consensus. Either the targets are
stale relative to the July collapse, or they embed a terminal multiple far above anything a
25%-gross-margin business supports. **Treat the target as uninformative.** A consensus
target 3.6x above spot is a statement about the analysts, not the asset.
