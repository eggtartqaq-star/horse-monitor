# MRVL — Management Quality Assessment (Matt Murphy & team)
**Date:** 2026-07-19 · **Analyst:** Peter, Management Quality (Dept. 2) · **Management grade: B+**

## 1. Promise-vs-delivery record

| Promise | Made | Delivered |
|---|---|---|
| AI revenue $1.5B FY25 / $2.5B FY26 (April 2024 AI Day) | Apr 2024 | **Beaten decisively.** FY25 AI revenue "substantially above" $1.5B; Murphy Mar 2025: "very significantly exceed our $2.5B target in fiscal 2026." FY26 data center >$6B (+46% y/y), custom doubled |
| Data center TAM $75B CY2028 → raised to **$94B, 35% CAGR** (June 17, 2025) | 2024–25 | Raised, not cut, through the 2025 drawdown |
| FY26 delivery | — | Revenue $8.195B, +42% (+45% ex-divested auto ethernet); Q4 FY26 $2.219B, $19M above guide midpoint |
| FY27/FY28 outlook (Mar 5 / May 27, 2026) | 2026 | FY27 growth >30%, Q4 FY27 >$3B; Q1 FY27 $18M above midpoint ($2.418B, +28%), Q2 guided $2.7B (+35%), FY28 target raised $1.5B to **$16.5B** |

**Blemishes:** three self-inflicted credibility hits in 2025 — Mar 5, 2025 stock −20% on "muted" guidance despite beating the AI target; May 6, 2025 postponed Investor Day citing "uncertain macroeconomic environment" (−6.5%); Aug 29, 2025 −18% on a data center miss ($1.49B vs ~$1.51B Street) and light Q3 guide, Murphy attributing custom-chip "nonlinear growth" to "lumpiness." Beats own guidance every quarter; repeatedly missed *market* expectations before re-accelerating.

## 2. Capital allocation scorecard
- **Inphi, ~$10B (2021): A.** Electro-optics/interconnect now the core AI franchise; interconnect guided +50%+ FY27.
- **Cavium, ~$6B (2018): B−.** Original end-markets underdelivered, but supplied the custom-silicon DNA behind today's custom compute.
- **Innovium, ~$1.1B stock (2021): B+.** Teralynx switching now a cited growth driver.
- **Divestiture: automotive Ethernet to Infineon, $2.5B all-cash, closed Aug 14, 2025 — A.** ~10x revenue for a non-core asset.
- **Buybacks — outstanding timing:** FY24 $150M → FY25 $725M → FY26 **$2.0B** (26.6M shares). Sept 24, 2025: +$5B authorization plus **$1B ASR** (~$75–93/sh) near the cycle low; stock $289–313 by June 2026. $5.3B remaining as of May 2, 2026.

## 3. Insider activity
- **Open-market BUY:** Murphy bought **13,600 shares at $77.09 on Sept 25, 2025** (~$1.05M), the day after the ASR — correctly timed (~4x since).
- **Sales:** routine 10b5-1 (plan adopted Dec 16, 2025): 30,000 sh Mar 26, 2026; 7,500 sh Apr 15; 7,500 sh Jun 15 at $288.97–312.71 (~$2.24M). Holds ~788,009 shares — small vs holdings; no cluster selling.

## 4. Governance flags
- **Executive churn during the ramp:** Raghib Hussain (President, Products & Technologies) resigned effective May 2, 2025. **CFO transition June 11, 2026:** Willem Meintjes out; succeeded by **Dan Durn** (ex-CFO Adobe, Applied Materials, GlobalFoundries) — who was Marvell's own **Audit Committee Chair** (resigned board Jun 10, 2026). Orderly (Q2 FY27 guidance reaffirmed) and Durn is high-caliber, but audit-chair-to-CFO is a mild independence flag; watch the new audit chair appointment. No restatements under Murphy (the 2015–16 accounting scandal was the founders' regime; Murphy hired 2016 as the fix).
- **Combined Chairman/CEO** (Murphy). **M&A overhang:** Bloomberg reported Nov 6, 2025 SoftBank explored a takeover (ARM tie-up concept); talks failed on terms, could revive.

## 5. What this team would do in a downturn
2025 was the live test: pruned a non-core asset at full price ($2.5B cash), bought back stock aggressively at the lows ($1B ASR + $5B authorization), the CEO bought personally, and raised — not cut — long-term targets. Expect counter-cyclical buybacks funded by portfolio moves and sustained custom-program R&D. Residual risk: hyperscaler custom-silicon concentration means a lost socket, not macro, is the real downturn scenario — buybacks can't offset that.
