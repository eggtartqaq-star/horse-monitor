## Summary & Grade

KLA is, on the numbers, one of the highest-quality businesses in the semiconductor complex and arguably in all of large-cap industrials/tech. It combines ~60%+ gross margins, ~40% operating margins, ~40% ROIC, >90% ROE, 30%+ free-cash-flow margins, and a near-perfect earnings-to-cash-flow tie-out, with only a modest, well-laddered debt load. Accounting quality is pristine: GAAP and non-GAAP sit within ~3% of each other (the opposite of a Marvell-type gap), operating cash flow slightly exceeds net income, and the share count shrinks rather than creeps. The single genuine caveat is **cyclicality** — this is wafer-fab-equipment (WFE) demand, which does contract (FY2024 revenue fell ~7%), so the elite returns are earned on a cyclical revenue base, not a smooth one.

**Fundamentals quality grade: A**
**Financial score (committee scorecard input): 91 / 100**

**The 3 numbers that matter most for the thesis:**
1. **ROIC ~42% (FY2025)** — durable, structurally elite capital efficiency; the clearest single proof of the moat translating to economics.
2. **FCF ~$3.75B, ~31% FCF margin, ~92% cash conversion (FY2025)** — earnings are real cash, not accruals.
3. **Net debt ~$1.39B (~0.3x EBITDA) with ~15x+ interest coverage** — leverage is a rounding error against cash generation.

## Profitability

### Revenue trajectory and cyclicality (fiscal years ended June 30)

| FY | Revenue | GAAP net income | GAAP diluted EPS (pre-split) | Net margin |
|----|---------|-----------------|------------------------------|-----------|
| 2021 | $6.92B | $2.08B | $13.37 | 30.1% |
| 2022 | $9.21B | $3.32B | $21.92 | 36.0% |
| 2023 | $10.50B | $3.39B | $24.15 | 32.3% |
| 2024 | $9.81B | $2.76B | $20.28 | 28.1% |
| 2025 | $12.16B | $4.06B | $30.37 | 33.4% |
| 2026 (9M) | $9.92B | $3.47B | n/a | ~35.0% |

- Revenue CAGR FY2021→FY2025 ≈ **15%**. FY2026 is tracking to a record: nine-month revenue $9.92B plus Q4 guidance of **$3.575B ±$200M** implies a full year ~$13.3–13.5B.
- **Cyclicality is real but shallow.** FY2024 was a down year (-6.6% revenue, -19% net income) during the WFE digestion; FY2025 rebounded +24%. KLA's process-control franchise and large installed-base service revenue cushion the troughs, so peak-to-trough drawdowns are far milder than for etch/deposition-only peers. Net margin held at 28% in the worst recent year — most hardware companies would kill for that as a peak.
- FY2026 quarterly cadence (GAAP): Q1 $3.21B rev / $1.12B NI; Q2 $3.30B rev / $1.15B NI; Q3 $3.415B rev / $1.20B NI — steady sequential growth, driven by leading-edge foundry/logic, advanced packaging (heading toward ~$1B run-rate) and the AI capex cycle. Q3 revenue +11% YoY, +4% QoQ.

### Margins

- **Gross margin:** ~61.9% GAAP FY2025; non-GAAP ~62%. Structurally 60%+ for years — a function of process-control's mission-criticality (yield economics), not commodity hardware. Best-in-class for capital equipment.
- **Operating margin:** ~40% (approximate; management/analyst characterization). *Caveat: I could not pin the exact full-year GAAP operating income to the dollar from accessible primary sources — the operating-margin figure is corroborated but not independently tied to the 10-K line item, so treat the precise number as approximate.*
- **Net margin:** 28–36% across the cycle (table above). Exceptional and remarkably stable given the top-line swings.

### ROE and ROIC (verified as historically very high)

- **ROIC:** ~37% (FY2024) → ~42% (FY2025) → ~43% (Q1 CY2026), per third-party calculations. This is the cleanest quality signal and it is elite — roughly 40% pre-tax-equivalent returns on invested capital.
- **ROE:** ~90% (FY2024 average) rising to >100% on a recent-quarter basis. **Interpretation:** ROE is flattered by capital structure — sustained buybacks have shrunk book equity and there is ~$5.9B of debt in the stack, so ROE overstates underlying returns. **ROIC (~40%) is the honest number**, and it is outstanding on its own.

### EPS trajectory

GAAP diluted EPS (pre-split) rose from $13.37 (FY2021) to $30.37 (FY2025), a ~23% CAGR — faster than revenue, driven by margin expansion and a steadily shrinking share count. The FY2024 dip to $20.28 mirrors the revenue trough. Post-split, FY2025 GAAP EPS ≈ **$3.04**; FQ3 FY2026 GAAP EPS of $9.12 (non-GAAP $9.40) ≈ **$0.912 / $0.940** post-split. Q4 FY2026 guidance: non-GAAP EPS $9.87 ±$1, GAAP $9.66 ±$1 (pre-split basis, as issued in April).

## Cash Generation

- **FY2024:** Free cash flow **$3.03B** — FCF/net income ~110% conversion; FCF margin ~31%. Notably, FCF exceeded a down earnings year, underscoring counter-cyclical working-capital release.
- **FY2025:** Operating cash flow **$4.08B**, capex ~$0.33B, free cash flow **$3.75B** — ~92% FCF/NI conversion, ~31% FCF margin.
- **FQ3 FY2026:** FCF $622.3M; trailing-twelve-month FCF ~$4B at a **31% FCF margin**.
- **Conversion quality:** OCF ($4.08B) slightly exceeds GAAP net income ($4.06B) in FY2025 — the gold-standard signal that reported earnings are backed by cash, with no accrual inflation. Capex intensity is low (~3% of revenue), typical of an asset-light, IP-heavy equipment model.

## Balance Sheet & Debt

**As of June 30, 2025:**

- **Liquidity:** Cash & equivalents **$2.079B** + marketable securities **$2.416B** = **~$4.49B**.
- **Debt:** Long-term debt **$5.884B**, with **no current maturities** (the $749.9M current portion present at June 2024 was retired; total debt fell from ~$6.63B at June 2024 to $5.88B at June 2025).
- **Net debt:** **~$1.39B** — i.e., KLA is *not* in a net-cash position, but net leverage is negligible at roughly **0.3x EBITDA**.
- **Debt profile:** Investment-grade senior notes in a laddered structure with long maturities and low coupons — e.g., 4.650% notes due 2032, 4.700% notes due 2034, and 5.250% notes due 2062, alongside older tranches. Fixed-rate, no near-term refinancing wall. Annual interest expense (~$250–280M) against EBIT of ~$4.5B+ implies **interest coverage of ~15–18x** — the balance sheet is effectively bulletproof.
- **Assessment:** A deliberately efficient, not stretched, balance sheet. Management runs slight net debt to fund buybacks while retaining an A-grade credit profile. This is a capital-allocation choice, not a risk.

### Capital returns (strong and quantified)

- **FY2025 total capital returned: ~$3.05B** = **$1.73B** buybacks + **$752.5M** dividends (~81% of FCF).
- **Dividend:** 16 consecutive annual increases through April 2025 (raised to $1.90/qtr pre-split); raised again to **$2.30/qtr pre-split** on March 12, 2026 (**+21%**), i.e., **$0.23/qtr ($0.92/yr) post-split**.
- **Buyback authorizations:** +$5B added in Q3 FY2025; a further **+$7B** authorized with Q3 FY2026 results — a multi-year runway of repurchase capacity.
- Track record: consistent double-digit dividend growth plus opportunistic buybacks that have shrunk the share count over time. High-quality, shareholder-aligned allocation.

## Accounting Red Flags

**None material. Earnings quality is top-decile.** Screen results:

- **Earnings vs. cash flow:** OCF ($4.08B) ≥ GAAP net income ($4.06B) in FY2025; FCF conversion 90%+ across years. No divergence — passes cleanly.
- **GAAP vs. non-GAAP:** FQ3 FY2026 GAAP EPS $9.12 vs non-GAAP $9.40 — a ~3% gap, driven mainly by acquisition intangible amortization and SBC, not aggressive "one-off" addbacks. This is the hallmark of a clean reporter (contrast with names where the GAAP/non-GAAP gap runs 30%+).
- **Share-count creep:** Absent — the count *declines* via buybacks; SBC is modest for the sector and more than offset by repurchases.
- **Capitalization games:** No evidence — R&D is expensed; capex intensity is low and stable.
- **Working capital / accruals:** No signs of receivables or inventory outrunning revenue in available disclosures. **Monitor item:** as a cyclical WFE supplier, KLA builds inventory ahead of demand and can see receivables swing with shipment timing; this is normal for the model but is the area to watch into any downturn. Not a red flag today.
- **Data limitation (disclosed):** Primary-source SEC/IR/aggregator pages were blocked by egress policy from direct fetch; figures here are drawn from KLA press-release content surfaced via search and cross-checked across quarters for internal consistency (quarterly sums reconcile to reported nine-month and full-year totals). The exact full-year GAAP operating-income line is the one figure I flag as approximate/unverified to the dollar.

## Financial Score: 91 / 100

**Rationale by component:**
- **Profitability (elite):** 60%+ gross, ~40% operating, ~40% ROIC, >90% ROE — full marks, minor haircut only because ROE is capital-structure-flattered.
- **Cash generation (elite):** 30%+ FCF margin, ~90%+ conversion, OCF ≥ NI — full marks.
- **Balance sheet (very strong):** ~0.3x net leverage, ~15x+ coverage, laddered low-coupon maturities — small deduction versus a pristine net-cash peer (KLA chooses slight net debt to fund returns).
- **Accounting quality (elite):** GAAP≈non-GAAP, no red flags, shrinking share count — full marks.
- **Cyclicality (the drag):** revenue can and does fall (FY2024 -7%); returns are earned on a cyclical WFE base. This is the primary reason the score sits at 91 rather than mid-90s.

**Grade A / Score 91.** Financial statements corroborate KLA's reputation as a top-tier compounder.
