## 2. Step 1a — Mid-cycle (through-cycle) operating profit

The central error available on this name is a single multiple on peak earnings. 2026E OP of ~₩269tn is 5.7x FY2025 and 13x the 2018 record; the 80% margin has no historical precedent (prior peak-year margin: 52%). So the valuation anchor must be through-cycle OP, built three ways:

| Construction | Method | Result (₩tn) |
|---|---|---|
| **A1 — last full cycle average** | mean OP 2021–25 (peak→trough→recovery; 2020 not obtained) = 16.5; mean 2018–23 ex-2020 = 7.0. Both ≈ **34–35% of the cycle's peak year**. | 16.5 in old-scale won — **not usable directly**: the business is 3–5x larger (bits, HBM, Yongin). Usable as a *ratio*: mid-cycle ≈ ⅓ of peak-year OP |
| **A2 — historical margin × scale-adjusted revenue** | mean OP margin of the six years obtained = **25%** (range used 25–35%). Revenue 2028 if bits grow ~20%/yr from the 2024 or 2025 base at *those* years' pricing = ₩137–168tn. | 137 × 25% = **34** … 168 × 35% = **59** |
| **A3 — peak ratio** | ⅓ × 2026E ₩269tn; range 28–40% of peak | **90** (75–108) |

A2 and A3 disagree by ~2x, and the disagreement is informative: A2 says "pricing normalises to 2024–25 levels," A3 says "this cycle's peak is proportionally like prior peaks." 巴爺爺's D&A trap argues for the lower end (₩30–40tn of D&A on ₩150tn of trough revenue); the LTA book covering >50% of 2027–28 capacity argues for the upper end. **I take a mid-cycle OP range of ₩40–100tn with a central ₩65tn** — roughly 24% of 2026E, 1.4x FY2025, and a ~30–35% margin on ~₩200tn of revenue. This is an estimate; its width is the point.

**Cross-check on the ratio:** FY2025 OP ₩47tn was itself a rising-price year (49% margin). A central ₩65tn therefore assumes the next mid-cycle is *better* than 2025 — justified only by volume growth (bits +20%/yr) and the HBM layer. If one believes the HBM layer earns no premium through the cycle (TrendForce: HBM per-wafer below DDR5 in 1Q26), ₩45–50tn is the right central and every value below falls ~15%. The sensitivity table (§7) covers this.
