## Headline

- **Intrinsic fair-value range (disciplined, through-cycle): ~$75 – $145 / share.**
- **Base-case fair value / target price: ~$95.** Probability-weighted: **~$80.**
- **Current price $212.75 is ~2.2–2.9x base intrinsic value.** Margin of safety is **deeply negative (≈ −125% to −190% vs base).**
- **菲比斯's demanded 25–30% margin of safety is NOT met — not remotely.** A +25% MoS to base fair value implies an entry near **$70**; even against the generous bull case ($142) it implies **~$106**. Both are far below spot.
- **Valuation score (committee input): 22 / 100.** This scores the *entry price*, not the business. The business is A-grade (巴爺爺 91/100); the price is the problem.

> **The one-line answer to Peterson's question:** At $212.75 KLA is *less expensive than its June peak, but it is not cheap.* On any required-return-based method it is expensive; only a "the sector 50x multiple is permanent" assumption makes it look fair. That is the MU/MRVL over-pricing error in a higher-quality wrapper.

## 1. Verified inputs & the multiple correction (READ THIS FIRST)

The committee has been working with a **"~42x forward P/E."** That figure is **split-polluted and understates the richness.** Anchoring to hard aggregates (net income and share count, which the split cannot distort):

**KLA trades at ~55–61x earnings, not 42x.** Historical KLA multiple ≈ 20–25x (Peterson). The stock is at **~2.5x its own history**. So KLA at 53–61x is *at a premium even to its already-extended peers* — the moat premium is real, but it is being paid on top of a sector-level re-rating.

Base 10.5% already gives the moat a generous ~1.5pt discount to CAPM. Per 菲比斯, this is a **cyclical** discount, not a moat discount — the moat is priced *in* the terminal multiple (26x base vs ~20x historical mid), not the discount rate.

## 4. Method cross-check — why DCF and current comps disagree by ~3x

| Method | Output | Read |
|---|---|---|
| **Gordon DCF** (11% WACC, 9%/5%/3.5%) | base ~$56–60 | Required-return discipline |
| **Exit-multiple DCF** (26x fair exit) | base ~$88 | Credits moat via terminal multiple |
| **Normalized P/E** (25–32x × FY27 $4.00) | $100–128 | Moat-premium to history |
| **Current-peer multiple** (48–52x × $4.00) | $190–200 | = "everything is at 50x" |
| **Reverse-DCF** | **needs ~25%/yr FCF growth for 10 yrs to justify $212.75** (WACC 11%) | Above KLA's *peak-cycle* 15% revenue CAGR |

The disagreement is **entirely the multiple/discount rate, not the cash flows.** DCF and normalized multiples say ~$60–128; the market price says ~$210. Bridging that gap requires believing *either* ~25% growth for a decade *or* a permanent 50x sector multiple + ~6% discount rate. I do not average the peer-multiple number in, because it is circular: it values KLA by reference to an equally-inflated AMAT/ASML, which is relative pricing, not a margin of safety. **Reality check on the low number:** base FV ~$74 ≈ ~$99B market cap — close to KLA's *pre-2026-run-up* valuation at a ~25x multiple. My DCF is not mispricing the franchise; the $284B cap is multiple expansion, and multiple expansion is not a margin of safety.

## 6. Margin of safety at $212.75

| Anchor | Fair value | MoS = (FV − price)/FV | Price vs FV |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bear | $34 | −526% | +526% |
| **Base** | **$74** | **−188%** | **+188%** |
| Bull | $142 | −50% | +50% |
| Probability-weighted | $79 | −169% | +169% |

- **菲比斯 demanded a 25–30% cyclical MoS.** On base intrinsic, the buy line is **~$52–56**; giving the franchise full credit at the bull case, **~$106**. Spot is **$212.75**. **Verdict: MoS requirement failed by a very wide margin.**
- To reach *even a 0% MoS* (price = base FV) requires KLA to fall ~65% to ~$74, or the base case to prove far too conservative (i.e., ~20%+ sustained growth).
- The +21% dividend raise gives a forward yield of only **~0.44%** — no valuation floor of consequence.

## 7. Valuation input to committee: **22 / 100**

This is a *price/entry* score, deliberately decoupled from business quality (巴爺爺 91, 菲比斯 WIDE/WIDENING, Elon overweight — all correct and un-contested here).

- **Why so low:** price is 2.2–2.9x base intrinsic; a deeply negative margin of safety; the price requires ~25%/yr growth for a decade or a permanent 50x sector multiple to justify; the "cheapness" is purely relative to a −30% peak and to equally-inflated peers.
- **Why not zero:** the underlying asset is genuinely elite; a Gordon/exit-DCF still yields a real, defensible intrinsic value; and there is a coherent (if optimistic) bull path to ~$142. This is *"wonderful company, wrong price,"* not *"bad company."*
- **What would move the score up:** a de-rate toward my range. **~$95–105 → score ~55–60** (base FV reached, moat premium intact). **~$70–80 → score ~75+** (菲比斯's 25% MoS met on base). A strong Jul 28 print that *raises FY27 WFE guidance* would lift the growth inputs but **not** rescue a 55–61x entry multiple by itself.
- **Recommendation to committee:** **do not initiate at $212.75.** If the committee wants AI-capex exposure, KLA is the highest-quality vehicle (agree with Peterson), but it is a *watchlist-and-wait* on price. Set alerts at **~$130 (enter watch/starter consideration)** and **~$95–105 (base-FV, size up)**. The Jul 28 earnings + Jul 29 FOMC cluster is a downside-vol catalyst that could deliver a better entry; there is no valuation reason to pre-empt it.
