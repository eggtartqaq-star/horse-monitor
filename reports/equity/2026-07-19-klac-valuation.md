## 3b. Steel-man of the bull / the street (audit fix #4)

The sell-side is uniformly *above* spot — **UBS $240 (Neutral), Morgan Stanley $274 (EW, PT
*raised* Jul 6), Susquehanna $275, Cantor $325** — and the audit correctly flagged that I dismissed
this as "bubble" without a fair hearing. The honest bull case:

- **This is the single best business in the firm's coverage.** 菲比斯 rates the moat **WIDE and
  widening**; 巴爺爺 grades the financials **A / 91**; ROIC is **~43%** (trough ~25%) — best-in-class
  process-control monopoly economics. Elite compounders *deserve* premium multiples.
- **The growth is secular, not just cyclical.** Advanced-packaging revenue is ramping to **~$1B in
  2026 (+57% from ~$635M)**, riding AI/HBM/chiplet demand that is structurally under-supplied;
  KLA management raised the WFE outlook to **>$140B** (its guidance, above SEMI's ~$135B).
- **Cheaper than ASML on forward.** At ~42x forward KLA is *below* ASML (~49x) and in line with
  AMAT — a rational relative-value buyer picks KLA within semicap.
- **The compound-into-the-multiple math is real.** A business durably growing EPS ~15%+ can grow
  into a 42x multiple in ~3–4 years without the multiple expanding at all; if the AI capex cycle
  has a long runway, today's ~42x forward looks like ~26–28x on FY30 earnings.

**Why I still discount it (on honest numbers, not name-calling):** (i) ~42x forward is still
**~1.9x KLA's own ~22x mid-history**, and history includes prior AI/EUV up-cycles — the market is
paying a full cycle-peak multiple *at* what management guides as a strong-WFE year, leaving little
error margin if 2027 WFE disappoints; (ii) ~24–30% of revenue is China-exposed to an escalating
export-control regime that the bull case treats as durable; (iii) "grow into the multiple" requires
**~15%+ EPS CAGR uninterrupted through a notoriously cyclical WFE market** — plausible in the bull
tail, not the base; (iv) the street targets are 12-month price targets on momentum/relative value,
not intrinsic-value estimates with a required-return floor. The bull case earns a **25% weight and
a ~$158 fair value** — a real, respectable path — but it is a *tail*, not the base, and even it
sits ~26% below spot.

relative pricing, not a margin of safety. But I now acknowledge the honest counter-point: **within
that relative-value frame, KLA is the cheapest high-quality name**, which is a legitimate reason a
sector-allocated buyer holds it — just not a required-return margin of safety for us.

- **What would move the score up:** a de-rate toward my range. **~$120–130 → score ~50** (forward
  ~26x, approaching the base exit multiple; watch-list starter). **~$95 → score ~60+** (base FV
  reached, moat premium intact). **~$70 → score ~75+** (菲比斯's 25% MoS met on base). A strong Jul
  28 print that *raises FY27 WFE guidance* would lift the growth inputs but not by itself rescue a
