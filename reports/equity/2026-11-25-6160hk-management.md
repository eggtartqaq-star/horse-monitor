**Reading the pattern:**
- **The positive reading.** Every dated profitability promise since January 2025 has been met or beaten, and the GAAP line arrived on schedule. That is the core signal, and it is strong.
- **The sandbagging reading.** Revenue guidance midpoint +10.3% from start of year to Q3; GAAP operating-income midpoint +66.7% [C]. An initial guide this far below outcome is a habit, not a forecast error. It makes guidance a floor, not a central estimate.
- **Order timing.** Management itself attributed **~$50M of Q3'26 US BRUKINSA revenue to order timing** [S: Earnings Whispers review of the Q3 call]. Volunteering it counts in their favour. It also means part of the Q3 beat borrows from Q4.
- **Q4 is guided down hard.** FY guide $1.2–1.3B less 9M $993.6M implies Q4 GAAP operating income of **$206–306M, vs $418.7M in Q3 (−27% to −51%)** [C]. Earnings Whispers reads the same arithmetic as "dropping by more than a third." On this team's record, the likeliest explanation is conservatism again.
- **The 2027 setup.** Management guided to "only modest margin expansion" for 2027 [S: Earnings Whispers, paraphrase, not verbatim]. **The 2027 guide is the next real test.** It is the first year without an easy SG&A-leverage tailwind (F, financials §4).

**Cross-check against the August package (L-001):** 巴爺爺 flagged that the FY2026 guide implied H2 operating income *below* H1. That implication is now **reversed**. Q3 alone was $418.7M, and the new FY midpoint ($1.25B) implies H2 ≈ $675M against H1 $574.9M [C]. The "conservatism" reading he preferred has been vindicated.

### 6.1 Redomicile to Switzerland
- **Mechanics:** a continuation from the Cayman Islands to Switzerland (legal personality preserved). Approved by shareholders 2025-04-28, effective **2025-05-27**. The board recommended it unanimously [S: S-4/A Jan 2025; investing.com 2025-05-27]. Registered office: Aeschengraben 27, Basel [F news].

## 7. Red flags (ranked)

1. **Founder selling without any offsetting insider buying.** $327.9M by the CEO and $380.1M by insiders in 12 months, zero purchases (§4). *Alignment, not integrity.*
2. **Board independence in substance.** Combined Chair/CEO, with the reported lead independent director affiliated with an 8% holder (§6.2).
3. **China tax and transfer-pricing tail.** One settlement after the redomicile. Whether open years remain is not obtained (§6.3).
4. **Guidance is set as a floor.** Three raises, a ~$50M timing-aided Q3, and a Q4 guided down 27–51%. **Valuation work should not take guidance as a midpoint** (§2).
5. **GAAP EPS will soon be inflated by a one-off.** Management says 2026 earnings "may provide sufficient positive evidence to reverse certain valuation allowances," producing a **material tax benefit** [S: Q3'26 release]. It is a legitimate accounting event, but it will flatter headline GAAP EPS in the reversal quarter. **Strip it out.**
6. **Undisclosed deal economics** (Revolution Medicines) (§3).
7. **Wide GAAP-to-non-GAAP gap.** FY2026 guide: $1.2–1.3B GAAP vs $1.9–2.0B non-GAAP operating income [S]. Composition not obtained [F fin. Flag 3].

**Integrity screen:**
- **No restatement found.** The tax settlement was explicitly not a restatement event [S].
- **No abrupt CFO exit.** The 2024 transition came with an orderly handover and "no disagreement" [S].
- **No blame-shifting language found** in the extracts I could see. Management volunteered the unflattering Q3 order-timing point.

## 8. What this management team would do in a downturn *(inference from the record, not a forecast)*

- **Protect the pipeline over the margin.** The team ran R&D at ~36% of revenue straight through the profitability inflection [F; S], and it chose to build a sales force instead of licensing out [F]. Expect R&D to be the last line cut.
- **Prune rather than spray.** The April 2025 TIGIT kill shows it will stop a programme on data. Expect cuts in solid-tumour and early-stage work before haematology.
- **Fund from the balance sheet, not new equity.** $5.10B cash [F], positive FCF, and a record of non-dilutive monetisation (Royalty Pharma $885M). Equity issuance is unlikely unless a transformative deal appears. That last case is the risk.
- **Guide conservatively and keep beating.** This habit would cushion a downturn quarter in the near term. It also means a guide *cut* from this team would be a strong negative signal.
- **No buyback support.** Repurchases are small (~US$14M in Q3 2026, aggregator-sourced) and offset only part of the dilution. Holders should not expect management to defend the stock. Expect the founder's 10b5-1 selling to continue on its schedule regardless.
- **Lean on its global footprint and the Swiss/US-manufacturing posture** if the China/BIOSECURE tape turns. The OMB BCC list is due **2026-12-18** [F news], and that is the next dated test.
