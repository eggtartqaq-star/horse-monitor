## 1. Summary & Grade

### Grade: **A− · 82/100**

This is the strongest set of financial statements Dept 2 has graded this cycle. I want
to be blunt that this conclusion runs *against* the house's recent negative streak
(L-003 anti-anchoring check in §10 — I looked hard for the bear case, and on the
statements it is mostly not there).

The central question the CIO asked — *where exactly is the profitability inflection?* —
has a precise, dateable, clean answer:

- **Q1 2025 was the first GAAP-profitable quarter** in company history: net income
  **$1.27M** on ~$1.1B revenue, versus **-$251.15M** in Q1 2024. Wafer-thin, but positive.
- **FY2025 was the first GAAP-profitable year:** revenue **$5.34B (+40%)**, GAAP
  operating income **+$447.1M** (from **-$568.2M**), GAAP net income **+$286.93M**.
- **H1 2026 shows the inflection compounding, not plateauing:** revenue $3.21B, GAAP
  operating income **$574.9M**, GAAP net income **$464.4M** — H1 2026 alone exceeded all
  of FY2025's net income by 62%, *and did so after absorbing a $60M one-off China tax
  charge*.

Cash flow **confirms** rather than contradicts the turn (FY2025 OCF $1.1B = 3.8x GAAP net
income — the healthy direction of divergence), the balance sheet is **~$4.1B net cash**,
and dilution over five years has been **~1.9%/yr** — far milder than the brief's premise
assumed.

**What keeps this off an A**, in order of weight:
1. I could not open a single primary filing.
2. **A China tax audit settlement of RMB446M (~US$60–62M) landed in Q2 2026** — reported
   as a settlement over "adjustments to previously filed tax returns", read by outside
   commentators as post-Golden-Tax-Phase-IV enforcement on R&D super-deductions and
   **related-party transactions**. Management calls it immaterial. It is small in dollars;
   it is not nothing as a signal about historical tax positions. (§7, Flag 1)
3. **The GAAP-to-adjusted gap is wide** — Q2'26 adjusted operating income $503M vs GAAP
   $325M; adjusted net income $444M vs GAAP $237M (**+87%**). FY2026 guidance carries a
   ~$700M gap between the GAAP and non-GAAP operating lines. (§7, Flag 3)
4. **Receivables grew 88.9% in FY2024 against 55% revenue growth**, and I could not
   confirm it normalised. (§7, Flag 2)
5. **Guidance implies H2 2026 operating income *below* H1 2026** on higher revenue —
   on both GAAP and non-GAAP measures — and nothing I could reach explains why. (§3)

### The 3 numbers that matter most for the thesis

1. **GAAP operating income $325M in Q2 2026 vs $88M in Q2 2025 (+269%).** Operating
   margin has gone -14.9% (FY24) → +8.4% (FY25) → 16.5% (Q1'26) → **19.1% (Q2'26)**.
   This is the thesis.
2. **BRUKINSA US sales $893M in Q2 2026, +31%** — ~52.5% of *total group revenue* from
   one molecule in one country. It is the engine and it is the concentration risk, and
   they are the same fact.
3. **R&D at ~36% of revenue while still earning a 19% GAAP operating margin.** Roughly
   double peer R&D intensity alongside a near-20% margin. The earnings power of the
   current commercial book is *understated*, not manufactured.

## 3. The profitability inflection — the central question

### Where it is, precisely

**The inflection point is Q1 2025** — the first GAAP-profitable quarter in company
history: **net income $1.27M**, versus **-$251.15M** in Q1 2024, on ~$1.1B of revenue
(+49%). ([BioSpace, Q1'25](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/beigene-announces-first-quarter-2025-financial-results-and-business-updates))

A precursor: management flagged **positive non-GAAP operating income in Q2 2024** — the
adjusted line turned roughly three quarters before the GAAP line. The order of events
(non-GAAP first, GAAP three quarters later, and the GAAP line *actually arriving*) is
the normal and honest sequence. This is not a case of an adjusted number papering over
a GAAP line that never followed.

### ⚠️ The one thing in the guidance that does not sit right

FY2026 guidance: **revenue $6.6–6.8B**, **GAAP operating income $1.0–1.1B**, **non-GAAP
operating income $1.7–1.8B**. H1 2026 already delivered revenue $3.21B and GAAP operating
income **$574.9M**.

That implies **H2 2026: revenue $3.39–3.59B (higher than H1) but GAAP operating income
$425–525M (LOWER than H1's $575M)** — an implied H2 operating margin of ~12.5–14.6%
versus 17.9% in H1. **Operating margin is guided to fall 3–5 points in H2 on a higher
revenue base.** The same shape holds on the non-GAAP line.

Two readings:
1. **Conservatism.** Guidance was raised twice in 2026 already (Q1: to $6.3–6.5B revenue
   / $750–850M GAAP op income; Q2: to $6.6–6.8B / $1.0–1.1B). A guide beaten and raised
   every quarter is being set low deliberately. **This is the more likely reading.**
2. **A real H2 opex step-up.** Combined R&D + SG&A is guided to **$4.8–5.0B** for FY2026;
   H1 R&D alone was $1,153.5M. If the opex guide is honest, H2 carries a materially
   heavier spend load — most plausibly R&D, given a pipeline moving into late-stage trials.

I cannot distinguish between these without quarterly opex guidance, which I could not
obtain. **This is the most important open question for the next committee update.**
Reading 1 is more probable; reading 2 is the one that would hurt. I flag it rather than
assume it away.

leverage is decelerating from here, as it must. *Absolute SG&A dollars for any 2026

### R&D intensity — the key lever, and it is still very heavy

| Period | R&D | % of revenue |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 2026 | ~$541M *(derived: $1,153.5M − $612.3M)* | ~35.8% |
| Q2 2026 | **$612.3M** | **~36.0%** |
| H1 2026 | **$1,153.5M** | **~35.9%** |

[Source: Q2'26 10-Q summary](https://www.minichart.com.sg/2026/08/05/beone-medicines-ltd-reports-strong-q2-2026-earnings-and-revenue-growth-in-latest-sec-10-q-filing/).
FY2026 combined R&D + SG&A guide **$4.8–5.0B**.

**36% of revenue going to R&D while still producing a 19% GAAP operating margin is the
genuinely impressive fact in these statements.** Most profitable large biopharma runs R&D
at 15–25% of revenue. BeOne earns a near-20% operating margin while spending at roughly
*double* peer intensity. Implications:

- The margin is **not** being manufactured by starving the pipeline. This is the opposite
  of the classic late-stage-pharma margin-harvest pattern, and it is the strongest single
  quality signal in the file.
- There is a large **discretionary** lever. Cutting R&D toward 25% of revenue would drop
  ~$700M/yr toward operating income. That is *downside protection on the earnings line*,
  not a plan — cutting R&D in oncology destroys terminal value.
- Reported operating income is therefore **understated** relative to the steady-state
  economics of the *current* commercial portfolio.

R&D is also the most likely explanation for the guided H2 margin dip in §3.

**No evidence of capitalisation games:** R&D is being expensed at 36% of revenue, which
is the opposite of the capitalise-to-flatter-earnings pattern. I found no reference to
capitalised development costs. (Under US GAAP this is largely mandatory, but the *level*
confirms there is no aggressive treatment happening.)

suppressing the GAAP line while cash economics are already well established. **It is the
inverse of the fraud signature.**

## 7. Accounting quality flags

Ranked by how much the committee should weigh them.

### 🟠 FLAG 1 — China tax audit settlement, RMB446M, booked Q2 2026 *(the one I nearly missed)*

On **26 June 2026** the company disclosed (8-K) that its **wholly-owned Chinese subsidiary
concluded a statutory tax audit and settled with the local tax authority, agreeing to
"certain adjustments to previously filed tax returns"**, producing an income tax payment
including surcharges and interest of **~RMB446 million (~US$60–62M)**. **No administrative
penalty** was imposed. The charge was recognised in **Q2 FY2026 US GAAP results** — i.e.
it is *inside* the $237M GAAP net income reported for the quarter. Management states it
will not materially affect financial condition or operations.

Sources: [TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/company-announcements/beone-medicines-faces-rmb446-million-tax-payment-in-china-sees-no-material-impact) ·
[stocktitan 8-K summary](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/ONC/8-k-be-one-medicines-ltd-reports-material-event-9ca8cdafd7ee.html) ·
[8-K summary, 26 Jun 2026](https://www.minichart.com.sg/2026/06/26/beone-medicines-ltd-reports-completion-of-china-tax-audit-with-no-material-impact-8-k-sec-filing-june-26-2026/) ·
[$60M net income impact, Q2'26 call](https://www.gurufocus.com/news/9029559/beone-medicines-ltd-onc-q2-2026-earnings-call-highlights-revenue-surges-30-to-17-billion-guidance-raised-on-brukinsa-momentum).

**My assessment — smaller than it sounds financially, larger than it sounds as a signal:**

*In favour of management's "immaterial" characterisation:* ~$60M against H1 GAAP net
income of $464M and a $5.1B cash balance is genuinely small. **No penalty was imposed**,
which is the single most important detail — Chinese tax authorities do levy penalties
where they find culpable evasion. A settlement with surcharges and interest but no
penalty reads as a technical/valuation dispute, not misconduct. The company absorbed it
and *still* raised full-year guidance in the same quarter.

*Against:* one commentary frames this as characteristic of post-**Golden Tax Phase IV**
enforcement targeting historical **R&D super-deductions and related-party transactions**
across the pharmaceutical sector
([extraction](https://www.moomoo.com/community/feed/with-half-year-net-profit-exceeding-3-2-billion-where-117071598518277) —
secondary commentary, not company disclosure, and I could not corroborate the
characterisation from a primary source). If accurate, the exposure is **transfer pricing
between the Chinese subsidiary and the group** — which for a company that has just
redomiciled to Switzerland specifically to optimise group tax structure is a live,
recurring category of risk rather than a closed one-off. I cannot tell from what I could
reach whether the settlement covers all open years or only some.

**Committee action:** treat as a resolved item with an unresolved tail. Watch for (a) any
further tax provisions in H2'26, (b) any uncertain-tax-position (FIN 48) reserve build in
the 10-Q, which I could not read. **Not a thesis-breaker at this size.**

**FY2024 is the flag: receivables +88.9% against +55% revenue — a 34-point gap.** DSO rose

### 🟡 FLAG 3 — The GAAP-to-adjusted gap is wide, and I initially under-weighted it

| Q2 2026 | GAAP | Adjusted | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operating income | $325M | **$503M** | **+$178M (+55%)** |

Adjusted net income is running **87% above GAAP**, and FY2026 guidance embeds a ~$700M
annual addback — roughly **10.4% of guided revenue**.

**Balanced reading.** In BeOne's favour: management **leads with GAAP** in the headline
releases, **guides on GAAP operating income** as the primary measure, and the adjusted
*gross margin* differs from GAAP by only 50bps (87.8% vs 87.3%, the difference being D&A —
a legitimate, disclosed, tiny reconciling item). The GAAP line is real and is the one
being sold. That is meaningfully better behaviour than the typical biotech.

Against: a $700M/yr addback is not a rounding error, and if the bulk is **stock-based
compensation** it is a genuine economic cost that the adjusted figures erase. **The
composition of the addback — SBC vs D&A vs intangible amortisation — was NOT OBTAINED**,
and without it I cannot say how much of "adjusted" is legitimate. Given the share count
grows only ~1.9%/yr (§6), I suspect the addback is weighted toward amortisation rather
than SBC, which would be the benign answer — **but that is a suspicion, not a finding.**

**Use the GAAP figures throughout.** This report's grade, margins and inflection analysis
are all built on GAAP. Any downstream valuation work by 𢦀鳩仔 that uses the adjusted
$3.84/ADS should be flagged as using a measure I have not been able to validate.

Two caveats, because absence of evidence in search summaries is not evidence of absence:

### 🟢 FLAG 5 — Share-count creep: absent

Diluted ADSs flat at ~118M across Q1 and Q2 2026; ordinary shares +1.87%/yr over five
years (§6). Nothing to report.

### 🟢 FLAG 6 — Cash flow confirms earnings

FY2025 OCF/NI 3.8x; FCF margin (17.6%) roughly double GAAP operating margin; H1'26 cash
