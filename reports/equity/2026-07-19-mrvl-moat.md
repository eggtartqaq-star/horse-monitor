# MRVL — Economic Moat Analysis
**Analyst:** 菲比斯, Moat Analyst, Dept. 2 · **Date:** 2026-07-19

## Moat rating: NARROW — ERODING (custom ASIC leg) / STABLE (optics leg)

## 1. The ROIC test — has MRVL ever earned its cost of capital including Inphi/Cavium goodwill?

**No. Not once (on a GAAP basis).**

| Fiscal year (ends ~Jan/Feb) | GAAP result |
|---|---|
| FY2022 | Net loss $(421.0)M |
| FY2023 | Net loss $(163.5)M |
| FY2024 | Net loss $(933.4)M |
| FY2025 | Net loss $(885.0)M on revenue $5.767B |
| FY2026 | Operating income $1,322.9M on revenue $8.195B — first materially positive GAAP year |

Invested capital as of Feb 1, 2025: stockholders' equity $13,427.0M + total debt $4,063.8M ≈ **$17.5B**, of which goodwill was $11,586.9M (rising to $13,883.5M by Jan 31, 2026) — i.e., the balance sheet *is* the Inphi (~$10B) and Cavium (~$6B) purchase prices. Even in record FY2026, GAAP operating income of $1.32B on ~$17–18B invested capital is roughly **7–8% pre-tax ROIC (estimate)** — still below a ~10%+ semiconductor cost of capital. FY2026 GAAP net income of $2.67B exceeds operating income by ~$1.3B, inferred to include non-operating/one-time items (likely the automotive Ethernet divestiture gain) — do not capitalize it.

**Important caveat for the committee (added per audit awareness):** on a *non-GAAP* basis, 巴爺爺 computes ROIC ~12–13%, which is at or above an ~11% narrow-moat WACC. The GAAP-vs-non-GAAP choice is therefore load-bearing. My rating leans on the GAAP record because (a) four straight GAAP loss years is the honest long-run picture and (b) non-GAAP excludes recurring SBC of ~$590.8M/yr. But a committee member using the firm's non-GAAP convention could reasonably argue the moat is "narrow and just now earning its keep" rather than "sub-WACC." I flag this as the single biggest swing factor in the bear case.

**Broadcom contrast:** AVGO FY2025 (ended Nov 2025): ~$64B revenue, ~77% gross margin, record ~67% adjusted operating margin, Q4 AI semi revenue $10.8B and FY2026 AI semi guidance of $56B. Broadcom earns wide-moat economics *through* an even larger goodwill load; Marvell has never converted its acquisitions into clearly excess GAAP returns. MRVL's non-GAAP gross margin of 58.9% (Q1 FY27) vs AVGO's ~77% is the pricing-power gap in one line. Broadcom: wide moat. Marvell: narrow.

## 2. Evidence by claimed moat source

- **Switching costs — custom AI ASIC design wins (bull claim: multi-year lock-in). Rating: NARROW, eroding.** Supporting: within a program generation, lock-in is real — Marvell + Broadcom control ~95% of the custom AI ASIC co-design market, Marvell holds ~20–25% share anchored by AWS Trainium and Microsoft Maia. Counter-evidence (bear substantially verified): Benchmark downgraded MRVL on conviction that Amazon's **Trainium 3 and 4 went to Alchip**; SemiAnalysis attributes the loss to poor execution on Trainium 2. **Note the news desk (修大哥) rates this socket loss "unresolved" — Murphy publicly denied losing business.** My read: SemiAnalysis corroborates a compute-die loss, but I am NOT assuming further undocumented losses; the base case models generational churn probabilistically, not a confirmed cascade. Net: switching costs last one generation, not multiple.
- **IP / switching costs — Inphi electro-optics DSP franchise. Rating: NARROW, stable (the real moat).** ~60–70% share of 400G+ PAM4 DSPs, held across the 800G→1.6T transition with the first 3nm 1.6T DSP (Ara) ramping Q1 FY27. Counter: Broadcom is a credible #2 with in-house CPO; NVIDIA controls the largest optics demand pool.
- **Cost advantage. Rating: NONE.** TSMC manufactures; Broadcom has superior scale.
- **Network effects / brand / regulatory barriers. Rating: NONE.**

## 3. Threat assessment — top 2 credible threats

1. **Generational socket rotation in custom ASICs.** Hyperscalers deliberately dual-source, own the RTL, and pay design-services margins (~40s% custom GM). Data center is 76% of revenue with heavy two-customer concentration.
2. **Co-packaged optics / linear-drive optics disintermediating the pluggable DSP** over a 5–10 year horizon (concentrated 2028+; NVIDIA keeping pluggables on Rubin-generation scale-up near-term).

## 4. Ten-year test
For MRVL to be stronger in 2036, ALL must hold: (a) custom silicon stays a design-partnership market; (b) Marvell keeps winning ≥1 flagship XPU per generation; (c) pluggable optics survive CPO/LPO in most links, or Marvell wins the CPO socket; (d) it converts scale into GAAP returns above WACC — never achieved post-Inphi. Four independent conditions, two outside management's control → fails a conservative ten-year test. Optics is the likely survivor; custom ASIC is a good-not-great design-services annuity re-won each generation.

## 5. Implication for 𢦀鳩仔's margin of safety
- Value MRVL as **narrow-moat, high-cyclicality, high customer-concentration**: demand a **35–40% margin of safety** to base-case intrinsic value (vs ~20–25% for a wide-moat name like AVGO).
- Model custom AI revenue with **explicit generational churn** (each flagship socket ≤50–60% renewal probability per generation); haircut custom gross margins to low-40s permanently.
- Give terminal-value credit primarily to the **electro-optics franchise**; apply a fade to DSP economics beyond ~2031 for CPO. Do **not** pay Broadcom-class multiples for the historical returns on capital. Any thesis at current AI-euphoria pricing is a growth bet, not a moat bet.
