## 1. CEO succession — RESOLVED, no longer a forward event

The tasking framed succession as a future dated risk. **It has already happened.**

| Date | Event | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-04-20 | Apple announced Tim Cook will step down as CEO and become **executive chairman**; **John Ternus** (then SVP Hardware Engineering) named next CEO. Board approval described as unanimous, following a long-term succession process. | Washington Post 2026-04-20; 9to5Mac 2026-04-20 (reproducing Apple Newsroom) |
| 2026-08-31 | Cook's last day as CEO. | 9to5Mac |
| 2026-09-01 | Ternus becomes CEO and joins the board. **Arthur Levinson**, non-executive chairman for ~15 years, becomes **lead independent director**. | Al Jazeera 2026-09-01; search summary |
| 2026-09-01 | SEC Form 8-K/A: Ternus base salary raised to **$3 million** on the Transition Date; FY2027 equity award with **target value $55 million**. | SEC 8-K/A (search summary; not read directly) |

**Ternus background (dated facts only):** joined Apple's product design team in 2001; SVP Hardware Engineering 2021–2026 (Wikipedia; Al Jazeera). Al Jazeera notes he has overseen launches across product lines including iPad and AirPods.

**Assessment.**
- *Process quality — positive.* A seven-week-plus public runway (announcement 2026-04-20, effective 2026-09-01), an internal successor with ~25 years at the company, and the outgoing CEO retained as executive chairman is the textbook orderly handover. No evidence found of a forced or abrupt exit.
- *Residual risks — new, not removed.*
  1. **Untested CEO.** Ternus is an engineering executive with no public track record as CEO, on capital allocation, on investor communication, or on services/regulatory strategy. Today he is ~2.5 months in. His first full-year guidance and first capital-return decision as CEO are the next dated tests.
  2. **Executive chairman overhang.** Cook as executive chairman plus Levinson moving from chair to lead independent director means the *former CEO now chairs the board that oversees his successor*. That is a weaker independence structure than before (when the chair was non-executive). Whether Cook's role carries operating authority, and for how long, was **not obtained**.
  3. **AI is the inherited problem.** Al Jazeera explicitly frames the handover against criticism of Apple's slower generative-AI rollout (see section 2).
- *What was speculation vs fact.* Pre-April 2026 press reporting on a 2026 Cook exit and Ternus as front-runner is now superseded by the official announcement; no need to weigh it further.
