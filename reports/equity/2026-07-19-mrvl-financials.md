## Summary & Grade: **B−**

Marvell is in a genuine AI-driven revenue inflection — FY26 revenue $8.195B (+42% y/y), Q1 FY27 $2.418B (+28% y/y), management guiding FY27 ~$11.5B and FY28 ~$16.5B — with low net leverage and record operating cash flow. But quality is mixed: GAAP profitability remains thin and noisy (Q1 FY27 GAAP NI $34.5M vs $718.0M non-GAAP), the FY26 GAAP profit was inflated by a one-off $1.8B divestiture gain, the balance sheet is dominated by $13.9B of acquisition goodwill (now including a $3.5B pre-revenue optical startup), FY26 FCF converted only ~57% of non-GAAP net income, and top-10 customers are 82% of revenue. Growth quality: high. Earnings quality: mediocre. Balance-sheet risk: low. Concentration/estimate risk: high.

- Portfolio churn: automotive ethernet sold to Infineon for $2.5B cash (closed Aug 14, 2025; pre-tax gain $1.8B in Q3 FY26); Celestial AI acquired Feb 2, 2026 ($3.5B total consideration); XConn acquired Feb 10, 2026.

## Profitability (GAAP vs non-GAAP — both, and which is honest)

**Q1 FY27:** GAAP gross margin 52.1%; non-GAAP GM 58.9%. GAAP op margin ~14% vs non-GAAP 35% (exact GAAP op-income line unverified). GAAP NI **$34.5M ($0.04)** vs non-GAAP **$718.0M ($0.80)** — a ~20x gap. Bridge: SBC $207.6M, acquired-intangible amortization $225.2M, plus a large non-cash increase in the Celestial AI contingent-consideration liability, partly offset by a gain on a forward stock purchase contract.

**FY26:** GAAP GM ~51%, GAAP op margin 16.1%; Q4 non-GAAP GM 59.0%. GAAP NI $2.670B ($3.07) — **includes the $1.8B pre-tax divestiture gain**; core GAAP earnings ~$1.0–1.1B (estimate, ex-gain). Non-GAAP NI $2.466B ($2.84, +81% y/y). FY25: GAAP **loss** $(885.0)M vs non-GAAP +$1.377B. FY24: GAAP loss $(933.4)M. FY23: GAAP loss $(164)M vs non-GAAP $1.822B. FY26 was the first genuinely GAAP-profitable year in four.

**Verdict on honesty:** neither figure alone is honest. GAAP over-penalizes (contingent-consideration remeasurement is non-cash; intangible amortization reflects sunk purchase prices). But non-GAAP materially overstates owner earnings by excluding **recurring** costs: SBC $590.8M in FY26 (~7.2% of revenue, rising — $207.6M in Q1 FY27 alone ~8.6%), treating a serially-acquisitive strategy's costs as permanently "one-off." Honest anchor: non-GAAP NI less after-tax SBC → ~$1.9B FY26 (estimate), cross-checked vs $1.40B FCF. **Use FCF and SBC-adjusted non-GAAP.**

**Returns (goodwill-inclusive):** ROE core FY26 GAAP NI ~$1.0–1.1B on equity $18.2B ≈ **~6%**; headline ~15% is gain-inflated — reject. ROIC (invested capital ~$19.4B: equity $18.2B + debt $5.0B − cash $3.8B): GAAP-basis ≈ **~6%**; non-GAAP-basis ≈ **~12–13%** (estimate). Even generously, ROIC only now approaches cost of capital — the Inphi-era goodwill has yet to earn its keep; the thesis is that FY27–28 growth fixes it.

## Balance sheet & debt

- Cash $3,843.6M at May 2, 2026; long-term debt $4,961.3M, short-term $0. FY26-end face value $4,499.9M laddered FY27 $500M / FY29 $1,249.9M / FY30 $500M / FY31 $500M / thereafter $1,750M. No wall.
- FY26 interest expense $187.3M; GAAP op income covers ~7x, non-GAAP ~15x. Net debt ~$1.1B, well under 0.5x earnings power. **Debt is not a risk.**
- Goodwill $13,883.5M at May 2, 2026 (up from $11,062.2M on Celestial AI +$2.82B, XConn) — ~⅔ of equity. Impairment risk if AI custom programs disappoint.
- Inventory $1,400.9M at May 2, 2026 (after +$374M Q4 build) — fastest-moving working-capital line; a revenue miss strands it. Receivables balance unverified (blocked source).

## Accounting-quality screen (red flags)

1. **GAAP/non-GAAP chasm is structural, not transitional** ($34.5M vs $718.0M Q1 FY27); recurring exclusions (SBC, $225.2M/qtr amortization).
2. **Contingent-consideration whipsaw (Celestial AI):** GAAP earnings hit harder the better Celestial performs; keeps GAAP EPS noisy through FY27–28; confirms a large earnout on the balance sheet.
3. **FY26 GAAP NI not comparable** ($1.8B Infineon gain) — quoting $3.07 as run-rate is wrong by ~2.5x.
4. **Customer concentration extreme and worsening with custom-ASIC mix:** top-10 = **82% of FY26 revenue**; two customers ≥10%. FY28's $16.5B assumes custom revenue more than doubles — externally unverifiable.
5. **Share-count creep** (~883M diluted; Celestial paid substantially in stock; SBC issuance outrunning $200M/qtr buyback).
6. **FCF conversion 57% of non-GAAP NI (FY26)** — acceptable only if working-capital build monetizes in H2 FY27.

## Financial score: **66 / 100**

Growth/trajectory merit 85+; balance-sheet strength 80. Deductions: structural non-GAAP inflation with SBC ~7–9% of revenue (−10); GAAP ROIC ~6% on goodwill-inclusive capital with $13.9B goodwill incl. a pre-revenue $3.5B deal (−10); 82% top-10 concentration + program-based custom revenue (−10); FCF conversion 57% and unverified receivables (−4). Score is an input, not a verdict.

**The 3 numbers that matter:** (1) FY28 revenue outlook ~$16.5B vs FY26 $8.195B — the thesis is a doubling in two years; (2) **82%** top-10 customer concentration; (3) **~57%** FY26 FCF conversion of non-GAAP NI.
