## Situation summary

KLA is the dominant supplier of semiconductor **process-control** equipment — optical/e-beam **inspection and metrology** used to find defects and measure structures across the fab flow (~55–60%+ global share in inspection). This product category is **directly named** in US BIS semiconductor-manufacturing-equipment (SME) controls, so KLA's China exposure is a first-order geopolitical variable, not a second-order one.

- **China revenue exposure (declining):** China (ship-to) was **42.8% of FY2024 revenue**, fell to **33.3% in FY2025** (fiscal year ended 30 Jun 2025, per 10-K filed 2025-08-08), and ran at **~24% in fiscal Q3 2026** (quarter ended ~Mar 2026). Management guides China toward the **mid-20s%** as a normalized share. The structural de-rating of China mix is already well advanced and partly de-risks the name versus 2023–24. (KLA IR / 10-K; Q3 FY2026 release 2026-04-29.)
- **Export-control regime — KLA equipment is IN SCOPE.** The Oct 2022 BIS rule and successors control 24 SME categories "including certain… metrology and inspection equipment for use with patterned 300mm wafers," with a **presumption of denial** for tools destined to advanced-node (sub-14/16nm logic, advanced DRAM/NAND) fabrication in China. KLA cannot ship its advanced process-control tools to Chinese advanced-node lines without a license that is presumed denied. (K&L Gates 2022-10-21; Baker McKenzie; KLA regulations.gov comment letter.)
- **What KLA still legally sells to China:** legacy/mature-node process control (greenfield trailing-edge capacity), plus licensed service/consumables. Management characterizes China growth as **slower than overall WFE, more greenfield than tech-upgrade**, and legacy demand as "pretty weak this year" with modest 2027 upside (Q3 FY2026 call, 2026-04-29). The AI/advanced-packaging cycle (WFE outlook raised to **>$140B for 2026**) is the growth engine, not China.
- **US-China truce — the key calendar risk clusters in Nov 2026.** The Nov 2025 Trump-Xi framework produced a one-year truce whose principal semiconductor-relevant elements **expire ~Nov 9–10, 2026**: (i) BIS suspended the **"Affiliates Rule" / 50% Rule** until **2026-11-09**; (ii) Section 301 exclusions and the 24% reciprocal-duty suspension run to **2026-11-10**; (iii) China suspended its **Oct 9, 2025 rare-earth/gallium/germanium export controls** for one year and terminated its antidumping/antitrust probes of US chip-supply-chain firms. (Federal Register 2025-11-12; White House fact sheet 2025-11; Morrison Foerster; Squire Patton Boggs.)
- **Customer concentration — Taiwan/Korea/US allied fabs.** KLA's demand base is TSMC (largest single customer / Taiwan is the largest ship-to geography), Samsung, SK Hynix, Intel, plus advanced-packaging/OSAT. This concentrates KLA in the **jurisdictions most exposed to a Taiwan-Strait shock** but also aligns it with the US-favored, non-restricted side of the export-control line.
- **VEU cross-read:** BIS revoked Validated End-User status for Samsung/SK Hynix/Intel China fabs (effective 2025-12-31) and will **not license capacity expansion or tech upgrades** in China (per `2026-07-18-regulatory-mu.md`). This caps KLA's tool sales into those specific China fabs even for allied customers — a mild structural headwind already in the run-rate.

## Escalation assessment

- **Truce non-renewal (the dominant near-term binary).** If the Nov 2026 elements lapse, the reflexive risk is a **Chinese rare-earth/gallium/germanium re-imposition** and/or **snap-back of the BIS Affiliates 50% Rule**, which would sweep many China JV/affiliate end-users into Entity-List treatment and force KLA to license-screen a far larger swathe of its China order book. Probability the truce is at least rolled/partially extended by ~Nov 1 2026: **~55–65% (est.)** given both sides' incentives; probability of a clean lapse into re-escalation: **~20–30% (est.)**.
- **Further SME tightening independent of the truce.** BIS has signaled a replacement framework for the rescinded AI Diffusion Rule "expected late 2026." Any tightening of metrology/inspection ECCN thresholds (e.g., lowering the node/parameter cutoffs, or restricting more mature-node process control) would hit KLA's *remaining* legal China business. Probability of incremental SME tightening touching process control in 12mo: **~25–35% (est.)**.
- **China retaliation naming KLA specifically:** low today — no unreliable-entity listing, no SAMR/MOFCOM action names KLA; the truce terminated the open probes. Probability in 12mo: **~10–15% (est.)**, rising sharply if truce lapses.
- **Taiwan-Strait tail:** low probability, extreme impact — see black-swan section. No change in reported PLA posture this cycle rises to imminent; treat as structural tail, not a base-case catalyst.

## Transmission to markets

1. **Direct revenue / order channel:** tighter controls or truce lapse remove incremental China process-control revenue and can trigger backlog cancellations/deferrals. With China already down to ~24% and guided to mid-20s, the *marginal* dollar at risk is smaller than in 2023; the tail is the **remaining licensed legacy business**, not the already-restricted advanced-node tools.
2. **Input-cost / supply channel (rare earths):** a Chinese rare-earth/gallium/germanium re-imposition raises cost and lead-time risk for KLA's own precision optics/e-beam supply chain and for its customers' fabs — an industry-wide WFE drag, transmitted via sentiment more than KLA-specific COGS.
3. **Risk-sentiment / multiple channel:** KLA trades as a high-multiple AI-capex proxy. Truce-lapse headlines compress the whole SME complex (AMAT, LRCX, ASML, KLAC) regardless of fundamental China dollars at risk — a de-rating, mean-reversion risk around the Nov 2026 calendar.
4. **Customer-concentration channel:** any Taiwan/Korea disruption hits KLA's demand base directly (TSMC/Samsung/SK Hynix), the most severe transmission path but lowest probability.

## Scenarios with tripwires (12-month horizon, prospective LONG)

**Base — "managed detente, China mix normalizes to mid-20s" (~55–60% est.)**
KLA's advanced-node China tools stay restricted (status quo), legacy China sells at a slower-than-WFE pace, and the AI/advanced-packaging cycle (WFE >$140B) drives the thesis. Truce rolled or partially extended past Nov 2026.
*Market impact:* neutral-to-positive; geopolitics is a known, priced discount.
*Tripwires (confirming base):* truce extension/rollover announced by ~Nov 1 2026; China revenue prints hold ~mid-20s in Q4 FY2026 / Q1 FY2027; no new BIS process-control ECCN change; WFE 2027 guide affirmed up.

**Adverse — "truce lapse + incremental SME tightening" (~25–30% est.)**
Affiliates 50% Rule snaps back (2026-11-10), China re-imposes rare-earth/gallium controls, and/or BIS lowers process-control node thresholds. KLA loses a chunk of remaining legal China legacy revenue; SME complex de-rates on sentiment.
*Market impact:* -15% to -30% drawdown in KLAC on multiple compression + China-cut estimates.
*Tripwires (early warning):* no truce-extension signal by mid-Oct 2026; Federal Register notice ending/modifying the Affiliates-Rule suspension; MOFCOM re-issuing rare-earth control notices; BIS "AI Diffusion replacement" rule text touching SME/metrology; KLA guiding China below mid-20s with a licensing rationale.

**Severe — "re-escalation to 2022-style broad controls or Taiwan shock" (~10–15% est.)**
Full breakdown: sweeping new SME controls (mature-node process control captured), Entity-List additions naming KLA China customers, Chinese retaliation naming KLA, or — tail within this bucket — a Taiwan-Strait military/quarantine event hitting TSMC.
*Market impact:* -35%+ for KLAC; SME complex and AI-capex names sell off together; TSMC disruption would be a global equity shock.
*Tripwires:* Entity-List additions of KLA customers/China ODMs; MOFCOM/SAMR action naming KLA; PLA blockade/quarantine reporting around Taiwan; US-China ambassadorial downgrade; export-license approvals for KLA halted wholesale.

## Black-swan candidates (low probability, extreme impact)

- **Taiwan-Strait kinetic event / blockade.** TSMC is KLA's demand epicenter and the world's advanced-logic chokepoint. Low probability on a 12-month horizon, but impact is catastrophic and non-linear. *Hedge:* the position itself cannot be made safe against this — treat via portfolio-level tail hedges (index puts, VIX, defense/energy offset), not KLAC-specific. *Early warning:* PLA exercise tempo/quarantine language, insurance-rate spikes on Taiwan shipping, US NEO (evacuation) advisories.
- **Rare-earth/gallium weaponization at scale** beyond the Oct 2025 template — a structural cutoff rather than a bargaining suspension — would raise SME production costs industry-wide. *Hedge:* rare-earth miners (MP-type) as a thematic offset; monitor MOFCOM licensing cadence.
