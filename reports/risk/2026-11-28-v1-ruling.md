## 3. The DAY stop

**Fact:**
- The CoreWeave stop was a stop-market order with DAY validity, placed at 01:13:27 EDT.
- It filled at 09:31:08 EDT at US$87.185, against a US$87.00 trigger [EV §2].
- Whether a GTC stop is available is unknown.

**Meaning:**
- The broker does not hold a resting stop. Protection at each open, where the gap losses happen, depends on the Owner re-placing the stop every trading day.
- A missed re-placement is not modelled [FC §4.4].

**Paper under KEEP (C-9 and F-8 practice), binding:**
- **D-1.** A DAY stop at the system stop counts as the C-9 broker stop only if:
  - it is re-placed every trading day, after 20:00 ET of the previous day (HKT 09:00 in winter, 08:00 in summer) and before the open, which is the window the CoreWeave order shows;
  - the order list shows it working before the open.

  The E2 daily raise (C-9) is done in the same step.
- **D-2 (F-8).** One working sell order per position.
  - Place today's stop only after yesterday's shows as expired or cancelled.
  - Two working sells for the same shares can short a margin account, which breaches `leverage: none` (R-7).
- **D-3.** An open position with no working stop at an open is an **S2**, logged the same day. N-11 then applies: no new entries until it is cleared with me. The signal ledger still records the system stop (K-4).
- **D-4.** With DAY-only stops, the Owner must act on **every trading day a position is open**, not only on entry and exit days. If he cannot, no new entries before that period (R-6).

**B′ (M-2): NOT MET on present facts.**
- M-2 requires a stop "working at every market open", of type "good-till-cancelled or the broker's equivalent".
- A DAY stop that needs daily action is not an equivalent.
- B′ stays OFF. At Webull HK it cannot be enabled unless a GTC stop is confirmed in writing.
