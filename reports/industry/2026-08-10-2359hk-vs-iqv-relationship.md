# 2359.HK and IQV — what the relationship actually is

**皮褸黃 (CEO), Industry Research · 2026-08-10**

> **Scope.** The Owner asked how WuXi AppTec (2359.HK) and IQVIA Holdings (IQV, NYSE)
> relate to each other. This is a *relationship* report, not a valuation of either name
> and not a recommendation on either. No department pipeline was run on IQV — this is
> CEO work off public sources, and IQV is **not on the watchlist**.

---

## The one-sentence answer

**They sit in different links of the same chain, they are paid out of the same wallet,
and they are becoming *less* competitive over time, not more** — because in December 2025
WuXi sold the one business where it competed with IQVIA head-on.

The common shorthand that both are "CROs" is the source of nearly every wrong conclusion
about the pair.

---

## 1 · What each company actually does

| | **2359.HK — WuXi AppTec** | **IQV — IQVIA Holdings** |
|---|---|---|
| Self-description | **CRDMO** — contract research, **development and manufacturing** | Clinical research + healthcare data, analytics and commercial services |
| Reporting segments | Chemistry, Testing, Biology | **Commercial Solutions** and **Research & Development Solutions** (restructured from three segments effective 1 Jan 2026) |
| Does it make drug substance? | **Yes — this is the business.** Chemistry was ~86.5% of 1H2026 revenue | **No.** IQVIA does not manufacture |
| Does it run large global clinical trials? | **No longer** (see §2) | **Yes — this is the business.** R&DS was $2,575m of Q2 2026 revenue |
| Does it sell healthcare data and commercial analytics? | No | **Yes** — Commercial Solutions, $1,793m in Q2 2026 |
| Listing | Hong Kong (H) + Shanghai (A, 603259.SS) | NYSE, single line |

A molecule moves discovery → preclinical → clinical → commercial manufacture → launch.
**WuXi owns the front end and the back end. IQVIA owns the middle and what happens after
approval.** The same drug programme can, and often does, pay both companies for different
work.

---

## 2 · Direct competition — real, but shrinking by design

There was exactly one place these two met head-on: **clinical CRO and site management in
China.** WuXi ran that through **WuXi Clinical** (CRO) and **WuXi MedKey** (SMO).

**WuXi sold both.** Definitive agreement with Hillhouse signed **26 October 2025**;
transaction **completed 16 December 2025**; consideration approximately **RMB2.8bn
(~US$400.65m)**, expected to generate roughly **RMB960m** of after-tax net profit. The
company's stated rationale was to concentrate on the core CRDMO platform.

**This is the single most important structural fact in the pair.** WuXi did not lose that
business to IQVIA — it chose to exit the category IQVIA leads, and redeployed into
chemistry and manufacturing, where IQVIA does not operate at all.

**What overlap survives:** laboratory testing and bioanalysis. WuXi's Testing segment was
**RMB2.48bn in 1H2026, +31.5%** — roughly 8.6% of revenue — against IQVIA Laboratories.
Real, but it is a minor line for both.

**Direction of travel: converging into complements.** Anyone modelling these as
substitutes is modelling 2023.

---

## 3 · The genuine shared driver — one wallet, two straws

Both companies are ultimately paid out of **biopharma R&D budgets.** That is the real
linkage, and it is stronger than the competitive one.

The measurement of that wallet is itself instructive: **the IQVIA Institute is the source
the market uses to size it.** Its own published work put biopharma funding at **US$82bn
raised across public and private rounds in 2025, down from US$102bn the prior year** — a
20% contraction. When that pool shrinks, it eventually shows up in both order books.

**So IQV functions as a gauge on 2359's demand environment.** Not a hedge — a gauge.

### Both instruments read positive right now, which is worth more than either alone

| | **IQV, Q2 2026** | **2359.HK, 1H 2026** |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | **$4,368m, +8.7%** (+8.5% cc) | **RMB28.897bn, +38.9%** (continuing ops +48.0%) |
| Order book | R&DS backlog **$34.2bn** at 30 Jun; net new bookings **$3.15bn, +19%**; book-to-bill **1.22x** | Backlog **RMB66.43bn, +25.2%** at 30 Jun |
| Near-term conversion | ~**$9.2bn** of backlog to revenue over next 12 months, **+7.5%** | not disclosed in this form |
| Guidance action | **Raised** — FY26 revenue $17,275–17,475m, adj. EBITDA $4,000–4,050m, adj. EPS $12.80–13.00 | **Raised** — FY26 revenue to RMB58.5–60.5bn from RMB51.3–53.0bn |

Two independent companies, on two different exchanges, in the same reporting season, both
raising guidance with growing backlogs. **That is a real signal about the demand pool**,
and it is more credible than either datapoint on its own precisely because the companies
do not share customers' spending decisions in the same way.

### But do not read WuXi's growth rate as the sector's

+38.9% against +8.7% is a **four-fold gap that the shared driver does not explain.** The
difference is WuXi's TIDES exposure — oligonucleotides and peptides, **RMB7.26bn in 1H26,
+44.3%**, riding GLP-1 manufacturing demand, where WuXi is reported to hold roughly a
quarter of the global clinical-stage market across 24 programmes.

**IQVIA has no equivalent exposure**, because GLP-1 economics for a CRO are trial services,
not tonnes of peptide. If you use IQV's 8.7% as "the sector rate" and conclude WuXi is
taking enormous share, you have mistaken a product-cycle for a market-share story.

## 4 · Where they genuinely diverge — jurisdiction

This is the only axis on which the two are meaningfully *opposed* rather than correlated.

- **2359 carries a US policy tax that IQV does not.** BIOSECURE was enacted 18 Dec 2025;
  WuXi was added to the DoD **1260H** list on 8 Jun 2026; a US federal court preliminarily
  **enjoined** that designation on 7 Aug 2026 and refused the Pentagon a stay. Live and
  unresolved. (Detail in `reports/news/2026-08-07-2359hk-regulatory.md`.)
- **IQV carries no equivalent designation risk.**

### The trap: "IQV is the BIOSECURE beneficiary" — it mostly is not

This is the theory most people reach for, and the evidence does not support it. The
beneficiaries consistently named in the coverage are **Charles River** (US preclinical),
**Lonza** (biologics and cell & gene manufacturing overlap with WuXi), **Samsung
Biologics** and Korean CDMOs, and **Fujifilm Diosynth**. Estimates of a **US$10–20bn annual
opportunity** for non-Chinese CDMOs and CROs circulate widely. **IQVIA is not named as a
primary beneficiary in any source I found.**

The reason is mechanical, and it follows directly from §1: **the work that would be
displaced from WuXi is chemistry and manufacturing, and IQVIA cannot take it.** IQVIA has
no plant to put it in.

**Actionable conclusion: if the Owner wants exposure to the BIOSECURE displacement thesis,
IQV is the wrong instrument.** That trade lives in CRL, Lonza, Samsung Biologics and
Fujifilm. Buying IQV to express it would be paying for a different business.

### And IQVIA is not an "anti-China" instrument either

IQVIA's own research reports **China's share of clinical trials rising**, and
**China-linked licensing and takeover transactions hitting an all-time high in 2025 — 94
deals, up from 71 in 2024**, with North American and European counterparties in 73% of
them. IQVIA earns fees from that activity. Shorting China exposure by owning IQV does not
work.

| First-half 2026 revenue | **US$8.52bn** (Q1 $4.15bn + Q2 $4.368bn) | **US$4.28bn** (the half in which it first out-earned Lonza's US$4.17bn) |
| Vendor P/E | 28.95 | not used — see warning |

**Read those two rows together.** *(The arithmetic below is mine, on the cited inputs.)*

> **IQVIA earns roughly twice WuXi's revenue at roughly half WuXi's market value.**

On price-to-sales: IQVIA is **~2.3x** FY26 guided revenue ($39.26bn ÷ $17.375bn midpoint).
WuXi is **~9x** ($78bn ÷ ~US$8.8bn; WuXi's own guidance implies the full year runs about
2.05x the first half, so doubling the half is a fair approximation). **Roughly a four-fold
difference in what the market pays per dollar of revenue.**

That gap is not irrational — WuXi is growing four times faster and expanded gross margin
9.4pp. **It does tell you precisely what is being bought:** IQV is priced as a
steady-compounder services business; 2359 is priced as a growth manufacturer in the middle
of a product cycle. They are not two ways of owning the same thing, and the pair is not a
"cheap versus expensive" comparison — it is a "different asset" comparison.

> ⚠️ **Do not tighten these figures without re-deriving them.** Vendor market-cap fields
> for 2359 span **HK$440bn to HK$609bn** — a 35% spread — because some count the H float
> only. The ~HK$600bn figure is the one consistent with the current price and the
> back-solved share count. FX convention: **HK$7.8/US$**, the HKMA band midpoint, stated as
> a house convention, not a fetched rate. On the A-share price the equity is about 5%
> lower, since the H line currently trades at a premium. **The four-fold multiple gap
> survives every variant of these assumptions; the precise multiples do not.**

## 6 · So how should the Owner use IQV in relation to 2359?

**As a gauge — yes.** IQVIA's bookings, book-to-bill and backlog conversion are a clean,
frequently-reported, English-language reading of the R&D spending pool that ultimately
funds WuXi. When IQVIA's book-to-bill deteriorates, that is information about WuXi's
future order book, available in a market where the filings are actually reachable.

**As a hedge — no, and I cannot even measure it.** Every market-data host in this
environment returns HTTP 403, so **no price series was obtained and no correlation,
beta or cointegration was computed.** Any claim that these two hedge each other would be
invented. They share a demand driver (positively correlated) and differ on jurisdiction
risk (negatively correlated), and **which dominates is an empirical question I have not
answered.**

**As a substitute — no.** Different segment, different growth rate, different multiple,
different risk. Owning IQV is not a lower-risk way of owning WuXi.

### What would change the relationship

| Watch | Why it matters to the pair |
|---|---|
| **DoD appeal of the 7 Aug injunction** | The only live mechanism that would force work out of WuXi. Would benefit CRL/Lonza/Samsung far more than IQV. |
| **OMB initial BCC list, due ~18 Dec 2026** | Independent route to designate WuXi that survives the 1260H injunction. |
| **WuXi 3Q2026 — watch backlog, not revenue** | First clean period containing the post-designation contract cancellations a federal judge found as fact. Cancellations leave the backlog first. |
| **IQVIA net new bookings and book-to-bill** | The forward read on the shared wallet. 1.22x in Q2 2026 is healthy; a fall below 1.0x is the sector warning. |
| **Biopharma funding trend** | US$82bn in 2025 vs US$102bn in 2024. Both companies are downstream of this number. |

## What I would tell the Owner in three lines

1. **They are complements that the market files under one label.** WuXi sold the only
   business where they truly competed, in December 2025.
2. **Use IQV as a demand gauge for 2359, not as a hedge or a substitute** — and note that
   IQVIA's own institute publishes the funding data that drives both.
3. **If the attraction was "buy IQV to play BIOSECURE", that thesis does not survive
   contact with the segment detail.** IQVIA cannot take manufacturing work. That trade is
   Charles River, Lonza, Samsung Biologics and Fujifilm.
