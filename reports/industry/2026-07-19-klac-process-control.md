## 1. Industry snapshot

Process control — the metrology (measurement) and inspection (defect detection) layer
of wafer fab equipment (WFE) — is the segment that verifies chips are built correctly.
It sits inside the broader WFE market alongside lithography (ASML), deposition/etch
(Applied Materials, Lam) and clean/CMP/ion-implant.

Current market data (all cited, dated):

- **WFE / total equipment cycle is in a record up-leg.** SEMI projects total
  semiconductor equipment sales to a record ~$139B in 2026 and a further record ~$156B
  in 2027, extending to ~$229B by 2028 (SEMI, late 2025 / 2026 forecasts). KLA's own
  framing: core WFE + advanced packaging rising from ~$110B (2025) to low-$120B core
  (2026), with the total incl. advanced packaging in the mid-$130B range.
- **Process control market ≈ $18B+** (metrology & inspection equipment, mid-2026).
- **Advanced packaging ≈ $11B and growing mid-to-high teens % YoY** in 2026 — faster
  than core WFE.
- **KLA FY2025 (ended June 2025): record $12.745B revenue, +17% YoY**, 62.8% non-GAAP
  gross margin, $4.4B FCF. **Q2 FY2026 (Jan 29, 2026 report): $3.3B revenue, +17% YoY,
  $8.85 non-GAAP EPS**, both ahead of consensus.
- **China normalization underway:** China WFE spend peaked ~$50B in 2024, ~$38B in 2025
  (-24% YoY), forecast ~$36B in 2026 (-5%). KLA's China revenue mix guided to
  mid-to-high 20s % (down from prior surge levels).

## 2. Life-cycle stage & evidence

**Stage: MATURE industry / GROWTH sub-segment (process control within it).**

The WFE industry overall is mature and cyclical — an oligopoly of a handful of suppliers
serving ~a dozen leading-edge fabs, with revenue that swings on capex cycles. But
**process control is the structurally advantaged growth pocket** inside it:

- **Process-control intensity rises with complexity, not wafer volume.** As nodes go
  below 5nm and designs adopt 3D stacking, chiplets, GAA transistors and HBM, the number
  of steps requiring inspection multiplies. Inspection/metrology therefore grows share of
  WFE across cycles — a secular mix-shift, not just a volume play.
- **KLA share is still rising in a mature market:** ~50% (2010) → ~56–63% (2024–25) of
  process control; >85% in optical wafer inspection. KLA reported +80bps process-control
  share gain and states it is now ~7.5x the nearest competitor.
- **Advanced packaging is the emerging growth curve:** KLA advanced-packaging systems
  revenue ~$950M in CY2025, +70%+ YoY, from ~$500M in 2024.

Verdict: treat KLA as a growth franchise riding on a mature, cyclical host industry.

## 3. Competitive map — WFE by segment

| Segment | Leader | Share / note |
|---|---|---|
| **Process control (metrology + inspection)** | **KLA** | ~56–63%; >85% optical inspection; ~7.5x nearest rival |
| Deposition / Etch / CMP | Applied Materials, Lam Research | Duopoly-ish; broad WFE breadth |
| Lithography | ASML | Monopoly in EUV / High-NA EUV |
| Process-control challengers | Applied Materials (~8–10%, share falling), Hitachi High-Tech, ASML (overlay/CD niche), Onto Innovation, Camtek (packaging inspection) | Fragmented tail |

**Defensibility read — KLA vs the other equipment makers:**

- KLA has the *narrowest but deepest* moat of the big-four. Its advantage rests on
  decades of defect-library/algorithm data, installed base, and the fact that a missed
  defect is enormously costly to a fab — switching risk is asymmetric. This is why share
  has *risen* in a mature market where challengers lost ground (AMAT process-control share
  fell from ~13% to <8%).
- ASML has a stronger *absolute* monopoly (EUV), but it is one product line exposed to
  litho timing and export controls. AMAT/Lam have broader WFE breadth but face genuine
  competition in each deposition/etch niche and more Chinese domestic substitution
  (Naura, AMEC, ACM growing 30%+ to ~$6B combined).
- **Pricing power ranking (my view):** ASML (EUV) ≈ KLA (process control) > Lam/AMAT.
  KLA's ~63% non-GAAP gross margin vs peers evidences this.

## 4. Emerging themes & inflection points

1. **AI leading-edge + HBM/advanced packaging = twin secular drivers.** CoWoS wafer
   demand forecast ~484k (2025) → ~678k (2026), +40% YoY. HBM3E stacks and 2.5D/3D bond
   interfaces require volumetric X-ray + sub-micron optical inspection — new demand pools
   for KLA. Advanced-packaging is the highest-growth line and KLA is entrenched early.
2. **Process-control intensity inflection at GAA + High-NA EUV nodes** — more metrology
   steps per wafer; durable mix tailwind.
3. **China normalization is a headwind, but a manageable one.** The 2023–24 surge is
   unwinding (-24% in 2025, -5% expected 2026); this is a *cyclical/geopolitical* drag on
   the China revenue line, partly offset by leading-edge/HBM strength elsewhere. Domestic
   Chinese process-control substitution is the slower-moving structural risk to watch.
4. **Supply constraints / DRAM cost inflation** trimming ~75–100bps off 2026 gross
   margin — a near-term cyclical friction, not a moat issue.

## 5. Where KLA sits — cyclical vs secular

**My thesis rests primarily on the SECULAR driver** (rising process-control intensity +
advanced-packaging), with a cyclical tailwind (record 2026–27 WFE up-leg) layered on top.

- Relative growth: KLA growing ~17% with expanding share while the host market grows high
  single / low double digits — i.e., KLA out-growing WFE and taking share simultaneously.
- Structural tailwind: yes (complexity-driven inspection intensity).
- Cyclical exposure: real — a WFE down-cycle would still cut KLA revenue; process control
  is *less* volatile than deposition/etch but not immune.

**Mid-July 2026 chip selloff context:** The SOX sold off hard in early/mid-July 2026
after a ~130% 12-month rally. Micron -13% in a session, Intel -21% over 7 days, Korea's
SK Hynix/Samsung -9–12%, and equipment names ASML/ASMI/BESI/Onto down ~6%+. Trigger was
sentiment/valuation (Meta "Meta Compute" reselling surplus AI capacity raising
supply-glut fears), **not a deterioration in WFE order fundamentals** — SEMI forecasts and
KLA backlog/lead-time commentary remained positive. Read this as a **valuation/multiple
reset on a still-intact fundamental cycle**, which improves KLA's entry risk/reward.

## 6. Sector view

**Process control (KLA's sub-segment): OVERWEIGHT — confidence MEDIUM-HIGH.**
**Broad WFE / equipment: OVERWEIGHT — confidence MEDIUM** (more cyclical, more China- and
sentiment-exposed).

Rationale: KLA combines the widest share moat in equipment with the best-positioned
growth pockets (advanced packaging, process-control intensity), demonstrated by rising
share in a mature market and peer-leading margins. The July selloff is a multiple reset,
not a fundamental break. I rate KLA's defensibility above AMAT/Lam and comparable to
ASML, with less single-product concentration risk than ASML.

### What would falsify this thesis
- **WFE orders roll over:** SEMI/foundry capex guidance cut, or KLA reports declining
  backlog / lead-times normalizing down — would turn the cyclical tailwind into a drag.
- **AI capex glut becomes real:** hyperscaler capex cuts (the "Meta Compute" oversupply
  fear materializing) reducing leading-edge + HBM build-out.
- **Share erosion:** KLA process-control share stops rising or Chinese domestic inspection
  tools (or AMAT/Onto/Camtek in packaging) take measurable share — breaks the moat leg.
- **China structural loss:** export-control escalation cuts KLA's mid-20s% China revenue
  faster than leading-edge growth can offset.
- **Margin structural decline:** gross margin falling below ~60% on sustained mix/cost
  pressure (vs. the ~75–100bps *cyclical* 2026 trim, which is acceptable).
