## 4. Lock-up expiry — actual terms

**The CIO brief's working assumption of 180 days / ~9 Jan 2027 is wrong. The lock-up was 90 days and has already expired, on 8 October 2026.**

- **[FACT] Duration: 90 days from the date of the prospectus.** F-1 and F-1/A prospectus language per search extraction of [SEC EDGAR F-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002120882/000119312526280172/d32785df1.htm) and [F-1/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002120882/000119312526289345/d32785df1a.htm) (pages themselves blocked). A KRX disclosure describes the ADS lock-up agreements as restrictions on disposing of shares and ADS for **90 days from the ADR underwriting agreement date** ([KIND filing, 24 Jun 2026](https://kind.krx.co.kr/external/2026/06/24/001006/20260624002216/10001.htm)).
- **[FACT] Who is bound:** the company and certain affiliates; the lock-up exhibit lists **SK square Co., Ltd.** among shareholders, directors and officers signing lock-up agreements ([F-1/A Ex-1.1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002120882/000119312526295501/d32785dex11.htm), snippet only). Barchart (via Yahoo) described it as "a 90-day selling restriction on the company and certain affiliates" ([Yahoo/Barchart](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/dear-sk-hynix-stock-fans-155945562.html)).
- **[FACT] Expiry date: 8 October 2026** — [Dealroom](https://app.dealroom.co/news/feed/sk-hynix-lock-up-period-expires-october-8-following-record-56b-q2-revenue), [Yahoo/Barchart](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/dear-sk-hynix-stock-fans-155945562.html), [Catacal](https://www.catacal.com/catalyst/sk-hynix-ipo-lockup-expiration).
- **[FACT] Early-release provision:** underwriters may waive; the company must announce any release by press release at least two business days before it takes effect (F-1/A Ex-1.1 snippet).
- **Staged expiry:** none found. NOT OBTAINED whether any secondary (longer) restriction applies to SK Square specifically beyond the 90 days; SK Square's stake is structurally constrained anyway — the offering was sized so SK Square keeps holding-company status (stake ≥ 20%), and the Aug 2026 KRW 40tn buyback-and-cancel raises SK Square's stake from 20.0% ([Herald, Aug 2026](https://biz.heraldcorp.com/article/10787130)).
