## 3. ADR–ordinary parity (0.1 × 000660.KS × KRW/USD)

**Headline: the ADS trades at a very large, structural premium to the Seoul ordinary — roughly 50% at the 6 Nov close — and the premium widened during the sell-off. This is the single most important ADS-specific fact for a holder at $129.**

**My computation at 6 Nov** [DERIVED]:

- FX: **no 6 Nov fix obtained.** Bracket: FRED DEXKOUS **1,333.99 on 30 Oct 2026** ([FRED](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/EXKOUS)); Trading Economics reports the won touched **1,353.00 on 5 Nov** and that level was still the reference on 9 Nov ([Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/south-korea/currency/news/597105)). Using 1,340–1,353:
- Implied ADS = **US$83.7–84.5**; actual ADS close **$127.34** ⇒ **premium 50.7–52.2%**.
- Cross-check at 30 Oct [DERIVED, lower confidence — Seoul 30 Oct close back-derived as 1,132,000 / (1 − 0.231) ≈ KRW 1,472,000 from TIKR's −23.1% week]: implied ADS = 147,200 / 1,334 = $110.3 vs actual $149.21 ⇒ premium ~35%. **So the premium went from ~35% to ~51% in one week**, because the ADS fell 14.7% while the ordinary fell 23.1%.

**Corroboration [DEPT]:** the technical desk independently computed a **44–56%** premium; the regulatory desk established the mechanism — **KSD cap on ADR issuance at 2.5% of shares, exhausted on 23 Jul 2026, with one-way conversion (ADS → ordinary allowed, ordinary → ADS blocked)**. I cite both rather than re-derive. Press background agrees on the mechanism: arbitrageurs cannot convert Seoul shares into ADS unless existing ADS holders first convert back; premium ran 16–51% in the first days (WSJ via [FnNews](https://en.fnnews.com/news/202607131111487920)), peaked ~37% and narrowed to 19% by mid-July ([Korea Times, 14 Jul 2026](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/economy/20260714/how-closely-will-sk-hynixs-adr-track-its-seoul-listed-shares)); "32% mystery" ([TheStreet Pro](https://pro.thestreet.com/market-commentary/sk-hynixs-32-mystery-why-the-shares-trade-at-a-huge-u-s-premium)); analysts expected the company might expand the conversion allocation once the ADS had a track record.

**What this means for the Owner [MY READ]:**
1. At $129 the Owner owns a claim on KRW ~113,000 of Seoul-priced equity (~$84) plus a ~$45 **scarcity premium that exists only because the conversion channel is capped**. If SK hynix or KSD lifts the cap, or if ADS holders convert and sell ordinaries in size, the premium compresses toward zero. That is a **−30% to −35% risk that has nothing to do with memory prices** [DERIVED: 84/129 − 1 = −35%].
2. Conversely, the premium is a cushion in sell-offs (ADS fell less than ordinary in the crash week) and it would widen further on any ADS-only demand shock — Nasdaq-100 inclusion in December is the obvious one (§6 item 7).
3. **Korean-side analyst targets cannot be converted to ADS targets at parity** (§7) — a KRW 1.6m target is $118 at parity but ~$178 at a persistent 50% premium. Anyone presenting a converted Korean target without stating the premium assumption is presenting a meaningless number.

## 8. The bear case at full strength

Stated as strongly as the sources allow, because the fundamentals in §5 and the targets in §7 are one-sided.

1. **The cycle is at or past its shortage peak.** Bloomberg Intelligence (Shuli Ren): shortage peaks **2Q26**, supply-demand balance **~4Q27**, possible oversupply **2028** ([24/7 Wall St, 6 Jul 2026](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/07/06/wait-michael-burry-is-right-about-the-memory-boom-ending-this-analyst-says-yes/)). Deloitte: big-three capex **+~340% 2024→2027**. Trefis: the 2–3-year lag puts the acute oversupply window in **2027–28** ([Trefis, 23 Jun 2026](https://www.trefis.com/stock/mu/articles/603858/micron-earnings-have-never-looked-better-start-worrying/2026-06-23)). SK hynix itself guides 2027 capex "significantly" higher. **Industry capex response is the mechanism by which every memory supercycle has ended, and it is now in motion.**
2. **The market has already told you twice.** Record 2Q → shares fell (29–30 Jul); record 3Q → ~−21% in a week. The multiples say the same: the three suppliers at **5.4–6.8x run-rate earnings** (Oct note) and SK hynix at **2.3x forward** ([TIKR](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/sk-hynix-down-57-high-162718085.html)) are **peak-cycle multiples** — the market is capitalising current earnings as transient. Equity peaks precede volume peaks because markets price capex inflections early.
3. **Terminal margin, not 2027.** Morgan Stanley's 2029–35 average OPM of **28% vs 53%** is the number that matters for a long-term holder; the 2027–28 numbers are not in dispute (MS *raised* 2028 by 1%). 4.9 GW of data-centre capacity slipping a year is the first hard datum of demand timing slipping.
4. **HBM commoditisation has started at the price level.** Samsung reportedly won **pricing parity with SK hynix on HBM4** after selling HBM3E ~30% cheaper ([TrendForce, 28 Jan 2026](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/01/28/news-sk-hynix-reportedly-to-supply-about-two-thirds-of-nvidia-hbm4-samsung-targets-early-delivery/)) — parity today is the precondition for undercutting tomorrow; TrendForce warns HBM prices "could enter an adjustment phase after 2026 as competition intensifies and capacity expands." The firm's Aug industry note put HBM margins at 2–3x DDR5; that spread is the thing being competed away.
5. **Samsung is catching up on every series.** Three-way HBM4 certification at Nvidia (June); HBM4E 12-Hi qualification (late Sept); HBM revenue share 21% → 33% in one quarter on Counterpoint's series. The counter-series (SK hynix ~62%) is from the same quarter and I cannot reconcile them — but **no series shows SK hynix's share rising.**
6. **China.** CXMT at 300–375k wpm by end-2026 is ~Micron-scale in commodity DRAM; 9.5% global DRAM revenue share already. HBM is two generations behind and DUV-bound, so the near-term hit is to **commodity DRAM pricing**, which is the larger part of SK hynix's DRAM revenue by bits. The 2027–28 capacity wave (Samsung P4/P5, SK hynix M15X/Yongin, CXMT) lands exactly when BI expects balance.
7. **The IPO priced at the top, and the ADS carries a premium that the ordinary does not.** Priced $149 on 10 Jul; peaked $199.87; below IPO price since at least 6 Nov; Seoul line **−57% from its high** ([TIKR](https://www.tikr.com/blog/sk-hynix-is-down-57-from-its-high-with-half-of-2027-and-2028-capacity-already-contracted)). On top of the cycle risk, the ADS holder at $129 owns ~$84 of Seoul-priced equity and ~$45 of conversion-cap scarcity (§3). **The company has every incentive to widen the conversion channel over time** (it was flagged in July as likely once the ADS had a track record), and when it does, the premium goes.
8. **Policy regime.** The 5–6 Nov crash was a policy shock. If post-midterm US rules on AI data-centre construction materialise, the 2028 "cliff" moves earlier. Foreign investors have already taken SK hynix holdings to a five-year low.
9. **The LTA shield is thinner than the headline.** ">50% of 2027–28 capacity under LTA" with **prices tied to market** is a volume guarantee. In a downturn, volume is not the problem; price is.

## 9. Anti-anchoring (L-003): the bull case at full strength, and whether I found it

**What would make this a good holding, stated at full strength:** an 80%-margin company, sold out through 2029 with 14 contracted customers covering more than half of 2027–28 capacity, leading the highest-margin product (HBM4, ~60–70% of Nvidia's allocation on every estimate) in a market where 4Q26 DRAM contract prices are still rising ~20% QoQ, returning KRW 100tn to shareholders including a live buyback, trading at 2.3x forward earnings, with Nomura at KRW 4.7m and an ADS-line consensus ~$245–250 (+70–95% vs spot), a probable Nasdaq-100 inclusion in December that would force index buying into a conversion-capped ADS line, and a buyer at $129 sitting at the 9th percentile of the 52-week range, 13% below the IPO price, after a sell-off caused by a US policy headline rather than by anything in the company's numbers.

**Did I find it?** The *fundamental* half, yes — every element above is sourced and dated in §5–§7, and it is as strong a fundamental picture as this desk has seen on any name. What I **did not find**:
- Any evidence that the **ADS premium is durable** — I found the mechanism and the July expectation that it would be *narrowed*, not sustained (§3). The bull case for the ADS at $129 is weaker than the bull case for the ordinary at KRW 1.13m by exactly the premium.
- **LTA price terms** — the one thing that would convert "sold out" into "margin-protected"; the only source on terms says prices float.
- **HBM's share of DRAM revenue**, the number that tells you how much of the 80% margin is HBM-mix versus commodity-DRAM price.
- Any **November-dated** evidence that SK hynix's HBM share is holding against Samsung.

So: a very strong operating story, a stock priced as if the story ends in 2028, and an ADS wrapper that adds a ~35% idiosyncratic downside the ordinary does not carry. The sources do not resolve which of those dominates the next twelve months, and I am not resolving it for them.
