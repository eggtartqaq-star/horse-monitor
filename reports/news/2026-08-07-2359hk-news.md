## 3. BIOSECURE ACT AND US POLICY — CURRENT STATUS

**This section is the most important in the file, and the answer is not the one the
2024-vintage consensus would expect.**

### 3a. BIOSECURE is LAW. It passed.

- **BIOSECURE Act provisions were enacted on 18 December 2025** as part of the
  **FY2026 National Defense Authorization Act**, signed by the President.
  (Sidley Austin, Dec 2025; Morrison Foerster 251218; Akin Gump; Ropes & Gray Jan 2026;
  K&L Gates 20 Jan 2026; Latham & Watkins.)
- **Critical change vs the 2024 draft:** the enacted version **does NOT name WuXi AppTec,
  WuXi Biologics, BGI, MGI or Complete Genomics** by statute. Instead a "biotechnology
  company of concern" (BCC) is defined as (A) an entity on the **DoD Section 1260H list**
  of Chinese military companies, or (B) an entity named through an **OMB-led process**.
  (Goodwin, Jun 2026; Arnold & Porter, Jun 2026.)

### 3b. WuXi AppTec was then put on the 1260H list — 8 June 2026

- **8 Jun 2026:** DoD published its updated 1260H list and **added WuXi AppTec Co., Ltd.**
  (Holland & Knight; Goodwin; FDA Law Blog; Fierce Pharma; C&EN.)
- **Company response, 8–9 Jun 2026:** WuXi called the designation **"mistaken and
  baseless"** and said it "will pursue all available remedies to correct this designation."
  It said it has no ownership, control or affiliation with any Chinese military or
  government body. (Endpoints News; Global Times; BioCentury — WuXi called the listing
  based on "demonstrably false" assertions.)

### 3c. The timeline is SLOW — this is the underappreciated fact

Per Goodwin / Arnold & Porter / Sidley summaries:

1. **OMB must publish an initial BCC list by ~December 2026** (one year from enactment),
   updated at least annually.
2. **OMB then has 180 days** to issue implementing guidance.
3. **FAR must then be revised**; prohibitions take effect **60 days after** that revision
   — "likely sometime in 2027".
4. **Pre-effective-date contracts are grandfathered for five years** from the FAR
   revision date.

**Consequence:** if the government uses the full statutory runway, **actual prohibitions
would not bite until roughly mid-to-late 2028**, and legacy contracts run five years
beyond that. (Holland & Knight framing: "The BIOSECURE Act Clock Is Running" — but it is
a long clock.)

**Scope, per the same sources:** the prohibition runs to **federal agencies contracting
with, or providing federal funding/grants/loans to**, entities that use BCC equipment or
services. It is **not** a blanket ban on US commercial pharma buying WuXi services. Latham
titled its alert "BIOSECURE Act Becomes Law **Limiting Grants** With Biotechnology
Companies of Concern."

### 3d. A US FEDERAL COURT HAS NOW ENJOINED THE 1260H DESIGNATION

**This is the breaking item and it materially changes the risk picture.**

- **11 Jun 2026:** WuXi filed suit against DoD in the **US District Court for the District
  of Columbia**, alleging the designation "inflicted substantial reputational, commercial,
  and operational harm" and threatened injury to its **~1,200 US customers** and hundreds
  of US employees at **four US facilities**. (Fierce Pharma; Global Times.)
- **29 Jun 2026:** preliminary-injunction motion filed. **22 Jul 2026:** hearing held.
  (東方財富, 10 Aug 2026, citing the company announcement.)
- **7 Aug 2026 (US time):** **US District Chief Judge James Boasberg granted a preliminary
  injunction**, barring DoD from "enforcing, implementing, or otherwise giving effect to"
  the 1260H designation while the litigation proceeds. The judge found defence officials
  **"repeatedly misread the evidence"** used to justify the decision.
  (SCMP, 2026-08-07/08; Reuters via Yahoo Finance; KFGO 2026-08-07; Bloomberg
  2026-08-08 "US Judge Grants WuXi AppTec Injunction on Chinese Military Company Label";
  Pharma Manufacturing.)
- The court **also refused DoD's request to stay the injunction pending appeal**, and
  required WuXi to post a bond of **US$1** (新浪財經 / 觀察者網 / 財聯社, 9 Aug 2026).
- **9 Aug 2026 evening:** WuXi announced the ruling to the market; A and H both gapped up
  10 Aug. (東方財富, 10 Aug 2026.)

**⚠️ CONTRADICTORY DATING — reported as contradictory.** Chinese filings and SCMP/KFGO
date the ruling **7 Aug 2026**; Bloomberg and Pharma Manufacturing headline it **8 Aug
2026**. This is almost certainly a US/Asia timezone artefact rather than two events, but
**I did not read the docket and cannot confirm it.** Assume "on or about 7–8 Aug 2026".

### 3e. What this does and does not mean

**Fact:** the injunction is **preliminary**, not final. The underlying case continues.
DoD sought a stay pending appeal and was refused — implying **DoD intends to appeal**.

**My read (labelled):** the BIOSECURE tail risk on this name has **not disappeared**, but
it has been **repriced down twice** — first when the enacted statute dropped the
named-company list and adopted a slow multi-year OMB/FAR process, and second when a
federal court enjoined the only mechanism (1260H) that currently captures WuXi. The
2024-era mental model — "BIOSECURE names WuXi, US revenue goes to zero" — **is factually
obsolete as of August 2026.** Anyone still carrying it will misprice this stock.

**The residual risks are real and I am not dismissing them:**
- The injunction can be reversed on appeal.
- OMB retains an **independent (B)-prong** to name a BCC in ~Dec 2026 **without** using
  1260H. A court win on 1260H does not block that route.
- Damage already done to customer relationships is not reversed by an injunction
  (see §5 — the judge said so explicitly).

## 5. CUSTOMER CONCENTRATION AND CONTRACT LOSS — YES, THERE IS EVIDENCE

**This is the item most likely to be missed by anyone reading only the results release.**

In granting the injunction, **Judge Boasberg found as fact** that following the 1260H
designation, WuXi's **customers and suppliers had "cancelled contracts, terminated
longstanding relationships, or moved their business to competitors."** He wrote that the
designation is **"inflicting harm on WuXi that later relief cannot repair"** and described
the listing as **"a scarlet letter [that] sends a clear message: keep away."**
(SCMP 2026-08-07/08; Pharma Manufacturing; Reuters via Yahoo Finance.)

Separately, **Bloomberg, 3 Aug 2026: "WuXi AppTec Sees Pentagon Tag Hurting Reputation,
US Business"** — i.e. **the company itself, in its own results communication, flagged
reputational and US-business damage from the designation.**

**Contradictory reads, reported as contradictory:**

- **JPMorgan** (via Futu News, June 2026): the 1260H addition "**negatively impacts
  sentiment, but actual business impact remains limited.**"
- **The court record and the company's own results-day commentary** say contracts were
  actually cancelled and relationships actually terminated.

**These are not reconcilable from public information and I am not going to pretend they
are.** Both can be partly true — real losses that are small relative to a book growing
48%. But note the asymmetry: **the 1H26 numbers that so impressed the market cover a
period in which the designation was live for only ~3 weeks (8–30 Jun).** The contract
cancellations Boasberg describes would land in **2H2026**, not in the numbers just
reported.

**⚠️ This is my single biggest flag for the committee.** The market on 4/7/10 Aug bought
a beat-and-raise that **structurally cannot yet reflect** the customer attrition a federal
judge has just found as a matter of fact. Named customers, contract values, and the
revenue quantum of the cancellations: **NOT OBTAINED — not disclosed publicly that I
could find.**

**Other concentration exposure:** WuXi is reported to hold **~25% of the global
clinical-stage GLP-1-related market** and to support **24 GLP-1 drug programmes**
(新浪財經, Aug 2026; earlier company statement cited 23 projects as of Sep 2025). That is
