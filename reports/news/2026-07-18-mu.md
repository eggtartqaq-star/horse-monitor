## Top items, ranked by materiality

### 1. Record FQ3'26 results and a far larger FQ4 guide (reported June 24, 2026)
- **What happened (reported fact):** Revenue of **$41.46B** (vs. $23.86B prior quarter and $9.30B a year ago, ~+346% YoY); GAAP net income **$28.24B** ($24.67/diluted share); non-GAAP EPS **$25.11** vs. ~$19.66 consensus; operating cash flow **$25.39B**; capex $7.1B; adjusted FCF $18.3B. Q3 gross margin cited at **72.6%** (up from 38% in FY25). FQ4 guidance: revenue **$50.0B ± $1.0B** (~14.5% above consensus), gross margin **~86%**, diluted EPS **$31 ± $1**. Data center revenue up more than sevenfold YoY to ~$11.5B. CEO Sanjay Mehrotra: results "reflect the strategic value of memory in the AI era."
  - Sources: Micron press release via [GlobeNewswire, June 24, 2026](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/24/3317151/14450/en/micron-technology-inc-reports-record-results-for-the-third-quarter-of-fiscal-2026.html) (also filed as [8-K Ex-99.1, SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000723125/000072312526000013/a2026q3ex991-pressrelease.htm)); [CNBC, June 24, 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/24/micron-mu-earnings-report-q3-2026.html); [StockTitan, June 24, 2026](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/MU/micron-technology-inc-reports-record-results-for-the-third-quarter-6f50161e5zxh.html); [Yahoo Finance, June 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/micron-nasdaq-mu-reports-upbeat-200852609.html); [Investing.com, June 24, 2026](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/micron-q3-fy2026-slides-record-415b-revenue-85-margins-93CH-4759286).
- **Why it matters (my read):** This is a memory supercycle of unprecedented magnitude — quarterly revenue and margins with no historical precedent for Micron. The single largest question for a LONG is not current demand but *durability*: guidance embeds pricing that history says mean-reverts. These numbers are so far outside historical ranges that primary-source verification (8-K/10-Q) is mandatory before committee.
- **Watch next:** FQ4 results (late Sept 2026); any intra-quarter DRAM/HBM spot-price data.

### 2. ~$100B in minimum contracted revenue under 16 strategic customer agreements, with price floors (June 24, 2026)
- **What happened (reported fact):** Earnings materials disclosed 16 strategic customer agreements (SCAs) representing **~$100B in minimum contracted revenue**, with **$22B of cash deposits/financial commitments** (~$18B cash deposits, ~$4B letters of credit). Most agreements carry price bands: ceilings near calendar-Q2 2026 market prices and floors said to guarantee gross margins **above any past cycle's peak quarterly margin**; when fully executed, fixed/ceiling-priced agreements would cover ~40% of revenue. Micron's 2026 HBM supply is reported fully committed under long-term agreements, with HBM4 ramping in calendar Q2 2026.
  - Sources: [Investing.com, June 24, 2026](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/micron-q3-2026-slides-record-margins-100b-customer-agreements-93CH-4759276); [BigGo Finance, June 24, 2026](https://finance.biggo.com/news/US_MU_2026-06-24); [Micron FQ3'26 prepared remarks (IR)](https://investors.micron.com/static-files/631b1a32-5537-46ae-8f40-82e42fc79dfe); [Seeking Alpha (HBM sold out for 2026)](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4881338-micron-technology-hbm-sold-out-for-2026-wall-street-is-still-underpricing); [Investing.com analysis](https://www.investing.com/analysis/microns-soldout-hbm-supply-makes-the-bull-case-hard-to-dismiss-200681391).
- **Why it matters (my read):** If the floor-price structure holds contractually, it directly attacks the classic bear case (cyclical price collapse) — the most bullish structural item in the sweep. Caveat (my read, not sourced): "minimum contracted revenue" definitions and escape clauses need Dept 2 legal/accounting scrutiny; outlets are paraphrasing company slides, and company-favorable framing should be discounted until the 10-Q text is read.
- **Watch next:** 10-Q disclosure language on SCAs ([10-Q, quarter ended May 28, 2026, SEC](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000723125/000072312526000015/mu-20260528.htm) — fetch blocked for me, needs direct pull).

### 3. China risk repriced: CXMT's $8.5B IPO and DRAM capacity fears knock the stock (July 15, 2026)
- **What happened (reported fact):** MU fell ~7% on July 15, below $910, as China's ChangXin Memory Technologies (CXMT) announced an **$8.5B IPO**; CXMT Q1 revenue reportedly surged ~719% YoY to RMB 50.8B (~$7.5B), and third-party projections have CXMT approaching Micron's DRAM capacity by 2026. Outlet interpretation (TradingKey): incremental Chinese supply threatens the pricing recovery behind Micron's margin expansion; counterpoint in same coverage — US sanctions keep CXMT out of advanced HBM, the highest-margin segment.
  - Sources: [TradingKey, July 15, 2026](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/262033102-micron-mu-stock-falls-cxmt-ipo-hbm-export-restriction-2026-tradingkey); [ts2.tech, July 2026](https://ts2.tech/en/micron-nasdaqmu-drops-more-than-cxmts-ipo-valuation-as-china-dram-risk-hits-shares/); [Yahoo Finance (SOXS +11% as Micron slides), July 2026](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/soxs-jumps-11-micron-slides-163330164.html); [KAD, 2026](https://www.kad8.com/hardware/chinas-dram-expansion-could-nearly-match-microns-capacity-by-2026/); [Crypto Briefing on CXMT](https://cryptobriefing.com/cxmt-us-export-control-challenges/).
- **Why it matters (my read):** This is the live bear thesis: commodity-DRAM supply from China caps the cycle even if HBM stays tight. Sources do not resolve how fast CXMT capacity translates to competitive leading-edge supply — I report that as unresolved.
- **Watch next:** CXMT IPO pricing/filing details; DRAM contract-price prints; Micron commentary on non-HBM pricing.

### 4. Washington: possible new HBM export restrictions; Congress pressure on Chinese memory (July 15-16, 2026)
- **What happened (reported fact):** On July 15, reports emerged that the US government is weighing **more restrictive unilateral export controls on HBM** (existing HBM-to-China controls date to December 2024). On July 16, House Select Committee on China Chairman Moolenaar (R-MI) and Rep. Whitesides (D-CA) wrote to Commerce Secretary Lutnick urging him **not to authorize US purchases of Chinese memory chips**. The MATCH Act (introduced April 2026) targets export-control loopholes around CXMT/YMTC/SMIC.
  - Sources: [TradingKey, July 15, 2026](https://www.tradingkey.com/analysis/stocks/us-stocks/262033102-micron-mu-stock-falls-cxmt-ipo-hbm-export-restriction-2026-tradingkey); [House Select Committee on the CCP letter, July 16, 2026](https://chinaselectcommittee.house.gov/media/letters/moolenaar-whitesides-to-secretary-lutnick-hold-firm-on-chinese-memory-chips-ban); background: [AI Frontiers on HBM control gaps](https://ai-frontiers.org/articles/high-bandwidth-memory-critical-gaps-us-export-controls); [BIS, Dec 2024](https://www.bis.gov/press-release/commerce-strengthens-export-controls-restrict-chinas-capability-produce-advanced-semiconductors-military).
- **Why it matters (my read):** Two-sided. Tighter unilateral HBM rules marginally cap addressable market but MU's China exposure is already constrained; blocking Chinese memory from US supply chains would be a clear MU positive. Market treated the headline bundle as negative on July 15 — sentiment, not settled policy. **Routing: The dictator (regulatory) and LeBron James (geopolitics) for follow-up.**
- **Watch next:** Any BIS rule text; Commerce response to the letter; MATCH Act progress.
