# Regulatory Brief — MRVL (Marvell Technology)
**Analyst:** The dictator (Dept. 4 — Regulatory) · **Date:** 2026-07-19 · **Status:** not held; prospective LONG under review. Companions: `reports/news/2026-07-18-regulatory-mu.md`, `reports/geopolitics/2026-07-19-mrvl.md`.
All probabilities are analyst estimates, not sourced figures. Investment-impact research, not legal advice.

## Net regulatory read for a prospective LONG: **mild net HEADWIND**
The inverse of MU's read on the same fact set: US industrial policy (tariff offsets, CHIPS/ITC) rewards owning US fabs — MRVL owns none — while MRVL's direct product exposure to today's China export controls is genuinely limited (custom ASICs aren't exported to China; interconnect-bandwidth control parameter removed). Two live items to watch weekly: the **Section 232 Phase 2 scope decision** (overdue since the Jul 1 Commerce report; the single most probability-weighted negative) and any **BIS move on networking/interconnect ECCNs** (lower probability, higher MRVL-specificity). China has not named Marvell — but 36% China ship-to revenue keeps the retaliation tail fat, and the truce clock (Oct–Dec 2026) is the calendar. The STAR Act design credit is the one identifiable upside option.

## 1. US export controls — apply asymmetrically vs MU
- **Applies to MU not MRVL:** Dec 2024 HBM FDPR rule is memory-specific; MRVL consumes HBM via TSMC CoWoS, an input, not a controlled export.
- **Indirect:** Jan 15, 2026 AC/S rule moved H200-class exports to China to case-by-case (25% fee, ~75K caps). MRVL's crown-jewel custom ASICs (Trainium-family, Maia 200) are US-deployed, not exported to China. Per CRS/BIS, the rule **removed "interconnect bandwidth" as a control parameter** — today's thresholds do not capture Marvell's interconnect/optical-DSP/switching silicon.
- **Live gap risk:** BIS extending controls to high-end networking/switching/optical-interconnect ECCNs is a discussed gap. **No proposed/interim rule found as of 2026-07-19** — speculation, not pending.
- **Revenue-share fee creep:** MRVL FY26 10-K flags that extending the China revenue-share fee beyond advanced GPUs could erode margin. Currently GPU-only; no extension found.
- Exposure: China ~**36% of FY26 revenue (~$2.97B, down from 43%)**, a substantial majority relocatable ship-to; genuinely at-risk in-China end-demand slice much smaller. Probability of new networking/interconnect controls in 12mo: **~20–30% (est.)**.

## 2. Section 232 tariffs — Phase 2 is the live binary, MRVL on the wrong side of the offset
- Phase 1 (25%, effective 2026-01-15) covers a narrow band of advanced AI accelerators. Commerce data-center report due **2026-07-01**; **no Phase 2 proclamation as of 2026-07-19** — decision live. Practitioner analyses expect possible sweep into analog/mixed-signal and **derivative products (servers, networking equipment)** paired with a **tariff-offset for US semiconductor production investment**.
- Mechanism: (i) imported hyperscaler custom-ASIC modules carrying MRVL silicon under covered HTSUS lines (classification unresolved; tariff lands on importer/hyperscaler, flows back as capex drag on MRVL-carrying programs); (ii) derivative-product coverage of networking equipment taxes MRVL-content systems broadly. **Marvell is fabless with no US fab investment to earn offsets** — a relative negative even where MU reads it as protective.
- Phase 2 scope plausibly Jul–Sep 2026. Some broadening: **~60–70% (est.)**; broadening capturing MRVL-relevant lines without usable offset: **~30–40% (est.)**.

## 3. China-side measures — InP input controls, retaliation toolkit, SAMR M&A gate
- China's indium/**indium phosphide** export controls (substrate for optics lasers/detectors): 6-inch InP wafer prices +~250% to ~$5,000, permit delays hitting the optics supply chain (partial easing mid-June 2026 per DigiTimes). MRVL DSPs are silicon CMOS but its optical franchise sits in InP-dependent module/laser chains — cost/availability input risk.
- **No Chinese action names Marvell** (no Entity/unreliable-entity listing, no SAMR probe); MOFCOM anti-dumping probe covers US **analog** ICs (final 2026-09-13), MRVL not in scope.
- Structural leverage: China SAMR approval gated Cavium (2018) and Inphi (2021) — any future large MRVL deal runs through Beijing. China formally targeting MRVL in 12mo: **~15–20% (est.)**, rising if the truce (elements expiring ~Oct–Dec 2026) lapses.

## 4. CHIPS Act & industrial policy — bypasses a fabless company; STAR Act is the option value
- MRVL has **no CHIPS award and is ineligible for the 48D fab ITC** (raised 25%→35% by OBBBA, signed 2025-07-04, fabs only). Industry pressing the **STAR Act** to broaden the credit to semiconductor **design/R&D** — introduced legislation, not law. Contrast MU (~$6.4B awards + 35% ITC on ~$200B US fabs). A design-side ITC would be a direct MRVL P&L tailwind. Enactment in 12mo: **~25–35% (est.)**.

## 5. Antitrust / M&A — Celestial AI closed; no open probes
- Marvell **completed Celestial AI on 2026-02-02** (~$1.3B cash + ~24.5M shares, ≥$3.25B total, up to $5.5B on milestones through FY2029); all approvals obtained; **no open antitrust/merger matter found.** Adjacent: POET Technologies faces a securities class action after Marvell canceled Celestial AI purchase orders (targets POET, not Marvell). New antitrust action naming MRVL in 12mo: **<10% (est.)**.

## 6. SEC / disclosure & litigation history
- 2019 SEC settlement: **$5.5M** penalty (no admission) for an undisclosed revenue "pull-in" scheme (FY2016 quarters, pre-Murphy). 2006–07 options-backdating class action settled **$72.5M** (final 2018). **Unverified current item:** an MRVL 8-K filed in 2026 surfaced under "settlement/shareholder class action" searches alongside an IR release "Marvell Technology Reaches Settlement in Shareholder Class Action Lawsuit"; direct fetches 403'd — **flag for 巴爺爺 to pull from EDGAR before committee.** No confirmed *open* securities case found.

## 7. Housekeeping — EU / other
No EU action specific to Marvell (demand-side exposure, low materiality). No US/EU trade-remedy case in MRVL product categories. FDA n/a.
