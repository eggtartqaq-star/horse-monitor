### 4 Nov 2026, +5.3%: midterm-election tech rally, not earnings
- [FACT] Gotrade, "Apple Stock (AAPL) Rises 5.3% as Big Tech Gains on Midterms (Nov 4)": close $349.51, +$17.61 (+5.31%), volume about 89.6m (roughly 2x normal). https://www.heygotrade.com/en/news/apple-aapl-stock-news-november-5-2026/
- [OUTLET] Analysts in the coverage tie the move to Democrats sweeping the 3 Nov midterms, with Apple seen as a "winner". The whole tech sector rallied.
- [FACT] Per the same search set, **AAPL fell on 3 Nov, the day after FQ4 results**: investors focused on slowing Services growth and lower projected margins. On 5 Nov the Nasdaq fell sharply and AAPL dropped about 1.3%.
- [FACT] Marketcapof, "Apple Stock Soars 5% in Best in 18 Months as Daily AI Caps Fall to $200 & Wall Street Splits on Targets". The headline names a second, company-specific factor (AI usage caps); see section 3. https://marketcapof.com/news/apple-stock-rally-ai-caps-analyst-targets/
- [READ] The 4 Nov day is mostly a **macro/political beta event**, not an Apple-specific re-rating. That matters: it is not evidence that the market liked the quarter. The quarter itself got a negative reaction on 3 Nov.
