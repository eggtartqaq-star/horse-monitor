### 3a. #1 — Revolution Medicines collaboration (10 Aug 2026) — HIGH

**What happened.** BeOne and Revolution Medicines announced a **multi-part collaboration**:
(i) a **clinical collaboration** pairing BeOne's **PRMT5 inhibitor (BGB-58067)** and
**EGFR×MET trispecific antibody (BG-T187)** with Revolution's clinical-stage **RAS(ON)
inhibitors** (initial combinations with **daraxonrasib** and **zoldonrasib**); and (ii) a
**regional rights agreement** giving BeOne **exclusive development and commercialisation
rights to four Revolution clinical assets in select Asian markets**. BeOne will **fund and
conduct a global registrational Phase 3** for one of the RAS(ON) inhibitors. Revolution is
eligible for **development and sales milestones plus tiered royalties** in the partnered
region.
Sources: [Revolution Medicines IR, 10 Aug 2026](https://ir.revmed.com/news-releases/news-release-details/revolution-medicines-and-beone-medicines-announce-clinical/) ·
[BioSpace](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/beone-medicines-and-revolution-medicines-announce-clinical-development-and-regional-commercialization-collaboration) ·
[Pharmaceutical Technology](https://www.pharmaceutical-technology.com/news/beone-revolution-medicines-oncology-therapies/) ·
[PharmExec](https://www.pharmexec.com/view/beone-medicines-collaboration-revolution-oncology-therapies)

**Why it matters.** RAS is the largest undrugged target class in solid tumours. This gives
BeOne Asian rights to four clinical RAS(ON) assets without discovery risk, and leverages
what management calls its in-house development "superhighway". It also **broadens the story
beyond haematology (BRUKINSA)** — the single biggest concentration risk in the name.

**Market reaction (reported fact):** Revolution Medicines **+6.2%** on the news
([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/healthcare/articles/revolution-medicines-rvmd-6-2-061454323.html)).

([SSE, 13 Aug 2026](https://english.sse.com.cn/news/newsrelease/voice/c/c_20260813_10828829.shtml)).

**My read (INFERENCE):** BeOne is the **payer** here (it funds the Phase 3 and owes
milestones/royalties). A market that had just re-rated the stock on *margin expansion* could
reasonably mark down a deal that **adds R&D spend**. **This is the closest thing I have to a
13–14 Aug fundamental irritant — but I want to be clear it is speculation.** The deal was

**Affected:** 6160.HK / ONC / 688235.SS; watchlist read-across to RVMD.

### 3f. #6 — BIOSECURE Act became law (18 Dec 2025) — MEDIUM, and under-appreciated

**Reported fact.** The **BIOSECURE Act was signed into law on 18 December 2025** as part of

entities that use such services, and from obligating grants/loans toward BCC procurement.

**Mechanism matters:** a **continuation** preserves legal personality — this was **not** a
merger or a new holdco. That is why the ADS, the HK line and the STAR line all survived

### 7.2 Stated rationale (company's words — reported)

To "better reflect its global oncology presence and prepare for its next phase of growth,"
moving to **Basel, "a global hub of biopharmaceutical innovation."** A company spokesperson:
*"This change enables us to further execute on our global growth strategy and reinforces our
corporate presence in Switzerland and our current European headquarters."*

### 7.3 Outlet interpretation (labelled — NOT the company's stated reason)

Press framing is consistently more direct than the company's: BeOne "is rebranding as it
tries to **diffuse geopolitical risks**"; the redomicile and the ONC ticker "reflect a
deliberate effort to **shed the perception of BeOne as a China-origin biotech** and compete
as a fully global oncology company"; and the move has "practical implications for **accessing
European capital markets and navigating geopolitical scrutiny of Chinese-linked life sciences
firms**" ([Pharma Letter](https://www.thepharmaletter.com/ones-to-watch/beone-medicines) ·
[SWI swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/healthcare-innovation/biotech-company-beone-medicines-relocates-headquarters-to-basel/89422392)).
