**[READ] on §2:**
- **Item 2.1 is the most important number in the release for MU and SK hynix.**
  - Nvidia added **~$111bn of commitments in one quarter.** Its own filing says this is mainly **multi-year memory procurement**.

  - Multi-year locked volume protects the buyer as much as the seller; the firm's industry report §6, indicator 8, flags price ceilings in post-2Q26 LTAs.

All of §5 is my own read.

### 5.1 MU
- **Positive:** Nvidia's $390bn of mostly memory commitments, with $203bn in FY28, is the strongest **demand-side** datum for MU since its 30 Sep print. It is consistent with Micron's statement that most CY2027 HBM is contracted at higher prices (firm file).
- **Missing:** nothing on Micron's HBM4 share at Nvidia, and nothing on price.
- **MU's +3.5% AH is beta to the print.** It is not company news. MU's own data come on **9 Dec** (capital-return step-up) and **~16 Dec** (FQ1, date not company-confirmed).
- **Technical distance:** MU at $737 AH needs **+12.3%** to clear the 11 Nov lower high of $827.58 (my arithmetic).

### 5.2 SK hynix
- **Positive:** the largest customer is locking memory supply multi-year; the volume risk recedes.
- **Negatives:**
  - **Customer governance worsened:** SEC civil probe; no permanent CEO.
  - 2027 HBM prices remain undisclosed, and a new CEO may press suppliers (TIKR, 12 Nov, firm file).
  - Washington is tightening China-adjacent compute access (16 Nov rule), on top of the Dalian tool hold-up (3 Nov Reuters, firm file).
- **Technical:** SKHY's AH print of $127.91 is back above the prior ATL of $122.20. This is **an AH print, not a close.** The re-entry note's trend-repair condition is for 老詹 to restate.
- **The decisive read is Korea's 18 Nov session** (opens 00:00 UTC). If the ordinary rises less than the ADS's +4.3%, the premium widens again, which is the risk flagged in the pre-close report §3.2.

### 5.3 Semis vs staples rotation
- **Direction:** the reversal trigger I named pre-close (an NVDA beat) fired in the expected direction: SMH +3.6%, XLP −0.5%, KO −0.5% AH.
- **Size:** small against an October rotation that was the largest since 2001.
- **The macro driver is unchanged:** 10-year ~5%; December hike priced ~90% as of ~13 Nov, relayed. **Staples remain rate-exposed. Semis remain long-duration.**

### 5.4 Trend change or relief bounce?

**Relief bounce until proven otherwise.** The reasons:
1. **The AH moves are small against the damage:** NVDA +4% after −27% in a month.
2. **Outlet AH quotes conflict:**
   - "Nearly 3% to $159.60" and "2.4%" (Decrypt / investinglive / AOL).

**Against that, the print is a fundamental datum.** It removes the demand-cliff fear for Nvidia's FY28 and for memory volume. That is more than a sentiment bounce.
