Q2-FY27 guide: revenue $2.700B ±5% (+35% YoY), non-GAAP EPS $0.93 ±$0.05 ([Marvell press release via Business Wire, 2026-05-27](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260527144543/en/Marvell-Technology-Inc.-Reports-First-Quarter-of-Fiscal-Year-2027-Financial-Results); [Marvell IR](https://investor.marvell.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1023/marvell-technology-inc-reports-first-quarter-of-fiscal-year-2027-financial-results)). Management raised FY27 revenue outlook to ~$11.5B and the FY28 target to ~$16.5B, ~$1.5B above the figure issued one quarter earlier; $3B quarterly revenue now expected by Q3, one quarter early ([TIKR, 2026](https://www.tikr.com/blog/marvell-technology-beat-q1-2027-earnings-and-raised-its-fy2028-revenue-target-to-16-5-billion); [Motley Fool transcript, 2026-05-27](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/05/27/marvell-mrvl-q1-2027-earnings-transcript/)).

Note the GAAP/non-GAAP gap (GAAP EPS $0.04 vs non-GAAP $0.80) is large — heavy stock comp and acquisition amortization (Celestial AI); Dept. 2 (巴爺爺) should scrutinize quality of earnings.

### 3. Custom ASIC competitive position vs Broadcom / Alchip — HIGH materiality (the core bull/bear debate; contradictory reporting, reported as contradictory)
- **Bull-side, recent:** KeyBanc raised its MRVL price target to $400 on Jul 14, 2026, citing custom AI silicon wins with Amazon and Google; analyst John Vinh expects Amazon's Trainium 3 to ramp in H2 2026, Trainium 3 Lite volumes into 2027, and Marvell to supply a Trainium 4 variant using network-processing-offload technology ([Benzinga, 2026-07-14](https://www.benzinga.com/analyst-stock-ratings/reiteration/26/07/60451482/why-is-marvell-technology-stock-gaining-tuesday); [24/7 Wall St, 2026-07-16](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/07/16/marvell-crashed-below-200-this-wall-street-firm-thinks-it-doubles-from-here/) — 44 covering firms: 38 Buy / 5 Hold / 1 Sell, consensus target ~$252.56).
- **Bear-side, unresolved:** In Dec 2025, Benchmark downgraded MRVL claiming it lost Amazon Trainium 3/4 designs to Taiwan's Alchip; Benchmark subsequently walked the claim back materially — saying Amazon added Alchip for additional design support while Marvell retained a position ([Sherwood News, Dec 2025](https://sherwood.news/markets/marvell-sinks-after-benchmark-cuts-company-saying-that-it-lost-its-amazon/); [Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com/stocks/marvell-earnings-ai-chip-sales-are-impressive-concern-over-market-share-loss-is-overblown) called share-loss concern "overblown"). **The sources do not resolve Marvell's exact Trainium 3/4 content; I am not resolving it either.**
- **Share context (secondary sourcing, treat as estimates):** Bloomberg Intelligence estimates cited by trade press put Broadcom at 70%+ of custom AI accelerator design services and Marvell at 20–25%, anchored by AWS Trainium and Microsoft Maia; the two control ~95% of the market ([Tom's Hardware, May 2026](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/semiconductors/custom-ai-asics-examined-from-broadcom-to-mtia); [Hashrate Index](https://hashrateindex.com/blog/design-partners-ai-asic-market-part-2/)).
- **My read:** This is the single biggest swing factor for a LONG. If KeyBanc's Trainium 3/4 content view is right, current FY28 guidance is credible; if the Alchip encroachment view is right, the ~$16.5B FY28 target has share-loss risk. Route to Department 2 for a design-win content audit before any committee vote.

### 4. Google in talks with Marvell on two inference chips — MEDIUM-HIGH materiality (context item, Apr 2026; not yet a contract)
- **Reported fact:** The Information reported (Apr 19–20, 2026) that Google is in talks with Marvell to develop two chips — a memory processing unit to work alongside TPUs, and a new inference-specific TPU — with MPU design targeted for finalization as soon as next year; **talks had not resulted in a signed contract** ([The Information, 2026-04](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/google-talks-marvell-build-new-ai-chips-inference); [Reuters via Investing.com, 2026-04](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/google-in-talks-with-marvell-to-build-new-ai-chips-for-inference-the-information-reports-4622218)). Context: this followed Broadcom locking a TPU agreement with Google through 2031 ([The Next Web, 2026-04](https://thenextweb.com/news/google-marvell-ai-chips-inference-tpu-broadcom)).
- **My read:** A signed Google program would be Marvell's third hyperscaler and the clearest rebuttal to the "Broadcom takes all" bear case. KeyBanc's Jul 14 note already credits a Google win — but the only primary reporting says *talks, no contract*. Treat any "Google win" claim as unconfirmed until Marvell or Google discloses. Watch item #1 for the next quarter.

### 5. Nvidia's $2B investment in Marvell + NVLink Fusion partnership — MEDIUM materiality (context item, Mar 31, 2026)
- **Reported fact:** Nvidia announced a $2B investment in Marvell on Mar 31, 2026; Marvell joins the NVLink Fusion ecosystem, supplying custom XPUs and NVLink-Fusion-compatible scale-up networking, with collaboration on optical interconnects, silicon photonics, and AI-RAN ([Nvidia newsroom, 2026-03-31](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-ai-ecosystem-expands-as-marvell-joins-forces-through-nvlink-fusion); [Marvell IR](https://investor.marvell.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1019/nvidia-ai-ecosystem-expands-as-marvell-joins-forces-through-nvlink-fusion)).
- **Outlet interpretation:** Tom's Hardware and The Next Web frame it as Nvidia buying "soft ecosystem lock-in" over the custom-ASIC challengers — a toll booth, not an endorsement ([Tom's Hardware, 2026-03/04](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/nvidia-invests-2-billion-in-marvell-whose-biggest-clients-are-trying-to-replace-nvidia-chips); [The Next Platform, 2026-03-31](https://www.nextplatform.com/connect/2026/03/31/the-2-billion-nvidia-deal-with-marvell-is-about-a-lot-more-than-nvlink-fusion/5213790)).
- **My read:** Strategically validating for Marvell's interconnect franchise either way, and a balance-sheet positive; but it does slightly complicate the pure "ASIC displaces Nvidia" narrative.

### 6. Electro-optics / interconnect build-out: Celestial AI closed, Polariton bolt-on — MEDIUM materiality
- **Reported fact:** Marvell agreed (Dec 2, 2025) to acquire optical-interconnect startup Celestial AI for $3.25B cash and stock, up to $5.5B on milestones ([CNBC, 2025-12-02](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/12/02/mrvl-earnings-q3-2026-acquires-celestial-ai.html)); the deal has since completed ([Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/news/4545457-marvell-completes-325b-celestial-ai-acquisition); [SDxCentral](https://www.sdxcentral.com/news/marvell-completes-325b-acquisition-of-photonic-startup-celestial-ai/); [Marvell newsroom](https://www.marvell.com/company/newsroom/marvell-completes-acquisition-of-celestial-ai.html)). On Apr 22, 2026 Marvell announced acquiring Polariton Technologies (plasmonics-based silicon photonics) to push optical links to 3.2T and beyond; terms undisclosed ([Business Wire, 2026-04-22](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260422119639/en/Marvell-Announces-Acquisition-of-Polariton-Technologies-Advancing-Optical-Performance-Scaling-to-3.2T-and-Beyond)).
- **My read:** Marvell is consolidating the optical scale-up/scale-across stack (DSP + photonics + Celestial's photonic fabric). This diversifies the thesis beyond XPU share — interconnect content grows with AI clusters regardless of whose accelerator wins. Integration and milestone-earnout dilution are the risks; no divestiture news found in the window.
