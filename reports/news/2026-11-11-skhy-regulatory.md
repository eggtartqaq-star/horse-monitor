### 1.2 Current licence regime for shipping US-origin tools to Wuxi (as of 11 Nov 2026)

**What is established (secondary, consistent across sources):**

- Wuxi operates under an **annual, quantity-limited BIS licence for calendar 2026**, granted 30 Dec 2025. The licence is for **operation and maintenance** — replacement parts, like-for-like tools — not for capacity expansion or node upgrades.
- **No 2027 licence has been reported as issued for any SK hynix China site as of early November 2026.** Reuters-sourced reporting (republished by [The Gazette, 3 Nov 2026](https://gazette.com/2026/11/03/sk-hynixs-chip-plans-hit-snag-over-u-s-restrictions-on-china/) and [Korea Times, 4 Nov 2026](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/world/20261104/sk-hynixs-new-york-chip-plans-hit-snag-over-us-restrictions-on-china-sources-say)) says the two sides are "negotiating a separate license for 2027 for equipment to keep up the Dalian facility" and that "it remains unclear whether SK Hynix can secure its 2027 permits by year's end." **The reporting is Dalian-specific; I found no Wuxi-specific 2027 statement either way.** Given both sites lost VEU in the same rule and were licensed together in Dec 2025, the working assumption is that **Wuxi's 2027 licence is on the same clock — a 31 Dec 2026 deadline, seven weeks out** — but that is my inference, not reported fact.
- **Commerce's position has hardened in public.** Under Secretary for Industry and Security **Jeffrey Kessler** "held to his position that the U.S. would not allow more American chipmaking tools to upgrade or expand capacity" at Dalian, though "the department has not formally denied" the Dalian expansion licence ([Korea Times, 4 Nov 2026](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/world/20261104/sk-hynixs-new-york-chip-plans-hit-snag-over-us-restrictions-on-china-sources-say)).

**The linkage that is now live — and it is the news of the past month:**

- **15 Oct 2026 (Reuters via TechCrunch):** SK hynix is reported to be **conditioning a new US NAND fab (Solidigm; upstate New York the lead candidate; aggregate reported at ~USD 45bn across phases in some coverage) on Commerce approving US tool exports to fill empty clean-room space at Dalian** ([TechCrunch, 15 Oct 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/15/sk-hynix-bargains-for-chip-equipment-exports-to-its-china-facility-in-return-for-building-a-factory-in-us/); [The Standard HK](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/innovation/article/345527/SK-Hynix-conditions-new-US-factory-on-more-US-chip-equipment-exports-to-China-sources-say); [Asia Times, Oct 2026](https://asiatimes.com/2026/10/sk-hynix-needs-a-trump-2027-deal-to-expand-its-dalian-nand-fab/)).
- **SK hynix's public denial, same report:** the company told TechCrunch it "is not seeking to expand capacity at its Dalian Mod B facility, which was completed in 2023." **The company statement and the Reuters sourcing conflict on what the licence application covers.** I record both and resolve neither.
- **3–4 Nov 2026:** Reuters follow-up: Commerce has withheld the Dalian licences; the New York plan "hit a snag"; no final investment decision on the NY fab ([The Gazette](https://gazette.com/2026/11/03/sk-hynixs-chip-plans-hit-snag-over-u-s-restrictions-on-china/); [Tekedia](https://www.tekedia.com/us-holds-up-sk-hynix-china-equipment-licenses-leaving-45bn-new-york-chip-investment-hanging/); [JKN](https://news.jkn.co.kr/editions/english/post/1068504)). **The USD 45bn figure appears only in secondary headlines; I did not find an SK hynix or Solidigm primary statement of that number — treat as unverified.**
- Market read-through: a Yahoo Finance piece reports **SKHY tracking its worst month since the Nasdaq debut** on this news flow ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/skhy-tracks-worst-month-since-034114532.html)). I did not obtain the price series; the committee should ask 老詹 / Tim Cook for the chart.

**Important distinction for the committee:** the October–November 2026 licence fight is about **Dalian (NAND)**, not **Wuxi (DRAM)**. Wuxi is the larger value exposure (§1.3), Dalian is the one in the headlines. They sit under the same legal regime and the same annual clock, so a Commerce posture hardening on Dalian is a leading indicator for Wuxi, but nothing reported says Wuxi's 2027 maintenance licence is in jeopardy.

### 1.3 Wuxi's share of SK hynix DRAM output; upgrade vs strand

**Share of output — the sources disagree, and the spread matters:**

**Working figure: roughly one-third of DRAM wafer output, falling as Yongin/Cheongju ramp.** The decline in the quoted share over time is itself informative — it is consistent with SK hynix diluting Wuxi by adding Korean capacity rather than removing Wuxi capacity. TrendForce expects SK hynix's China share of DRAM output to settle near Samsung's **30–35%** ([TrendForce, 30 Dec 2025](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2025/12/30/news-samsung-sk-hynix-escape-worst-case-as-china-veu-ends-annual-review-risks-loom)).

**Node status — the operational crux:**

- **Wuxi has reportedly completed conversion to the 1a (fourth-gen 10nm-class) node** despite the controls ([DigiTimes, 16 Jan 2026](https://www.digitimes.com/news/a20260116VL202/sk-hynix-dram-plant-production-euv.html)). 1a is the last SK hynix DRAM node that does not require EUV lithography on the critical layers in volume.
- **No major process transition is expected at Wuxi in 2026** (no 1b); **1b and 1c are reserved for Icheon and Cheongju** (Hankyung via [TrendForce](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2025/12/30/news-samsung-sk-hynix-escape-worst-case-as-china-veu-ends-annual-review-risks-loom)).
- Analyst view: Wuxi "will face difficulty in migrating beyond 1a nm because no EUV tools can ship to China" ([Nomad Semi](https://www.nomadsemi.com/p/memory-shortage-and-asml)). EUV export to China is restricted by Dutch and US rules independent of VEU; VEU revocation closes the non-EUV upgrade route too.
- **Capex at Wuxi is still rising:** KRW 581bn in 2025 at the Wuxi DRAM plant, +102% from KRW 287.3bn in 2024 ([Seoul Economic Daily, 25 Mar 2026](https://en.sedaily.com/finance/2026/03/25/samsung-sk-hynix-pour-15-trillion-won-into-china-plants-to-20260325)). That is maintenance-and-1a-conversion scale, not new-fab scale; it tells you the company is sweating the asset, not abandoning it.

**Upgrade-vs-strand cost: NOT OBTAINED.** I found **no analyst or company estimate** of the write-down or replacement cost if Wuxi is frozen at 1a. What can be said structurally, without inventing a number:

1. Wuxi is now a **legacy/mainstream DRAM fab by construction**: 1a-node DDR4/DDR5/LPDDR5 and similar, not HBM base dies (HBM3E/HBM4 base dies are 1b/1c, Korea-only) and not the leading-edge server DDR5 bins. It is, in effect, SK hynix's **commodity-DRAM profit centre in a shortage** — and §2/§3 of `reports/industry/2026-08-21-msft-industry.md` records DRAM contract prices up 25–29% q/q and shortage expected through 2030. In the current cycle, a frozen-at-1a fab is a cash machine, not a stranded asset.
2. The stranding risk is **back-ended**: it becomes material when 1a output can no longer be sold at acceptable margins, i.e. when the cycle turns or when customers require 1b+ parts. SK hynix's answer is the **Yongin cluster** (reported +360k wafers/month DRAM, [TechTimes, Jun 2026](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/317859/20260606/sk-hynix-dram-capacity-roadmap-revealed-yongin-alone-adds-360k-wafers-monthly.htm)) — new Korean capacity that progressively makes Wuxi a smaller share of the whole.
3. The **acute** risk is not stranding but **non-renewal of the annual maintenance licence**, which would degrade Wuxi output within quarters as tools fail and cannot be serviced with US-origin parts. That is the 31 Dec 2026 tripwire (§7).

### 1.4 Assessment

**Status: in force (final rule, Aug 2025, effective 31 Dec 2025); mitigated by an annual licence that expires 31 Dec 2026; 2027 renewal not reported as granted.** Probability-weighted view (my estimate, not a sourced figure): maintenance-licence renewal for Wuxi is the base case — Commerce renewed in Dec 2025 one day before lapse, Kessler's stated red line is *expansion/upgrade*, not *operation*, and the Reuters sourcing describes the 2027 maintenance licence as under negotiation rather than refused. The adverse case is a late, reduced or conditional renewal used as leverage in the Dalian/New York negotiation. The exposure is **operational and dated**, exactly as the brief framed it.

### 5.2 Foreign private issuer status and reporting obligations

- The listing was executed on **Form F-1** with an **F-6** for the depositary programme — the foreign-private-issuer route ([Strasmore](https://www.strasmore.com/blog/sk-hynix-nasdaq-debut)). Post-listing current reports are on **Form 6-K** (an interim-review 6-K for the period to 30 Jun 2026 is on EDGAR — [SEC 6-K index (not fetched)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0002120882/000119312526354777/d147827d6k.htm)).
- **What FPI status means, in general terms (not a legal opinion):** annual report on **Form 20-F** (due within four months of fiscal year-end, so **by 30 Apr 2027** for FY2026); no 10-Q/8-K; no Regulation FD, Section 16 insider reporting or US proxy rules; home-country governance permitted in place of most Nasdaq governance rules with disclosure of differences; financial statements may be under **IFRS as issued by the IASB** without US GAAP reconciliation (SK hynix reports under K-IFRS; whether the 20-F will be IFRS-IASB or K-IFRS reconciled is **not obtained**).
- **What it does not mean:** Sarbanes-Oxley §302/§404 certification and PCAOB-registered auditor requirements apply to FPIs. Antifraud liability (Rule 10b-5) applies in full.
- **FPI status is tested annually (last business day of Q2).** SK hynix comfortably satisfies the test (majority of shares held outside the US; management and assets in Korea). No risk of losing FPI status is identified.

### 5.5 Litigation disclosed in the F-1/A — US DRAM antitrust class action

- **Case:** *No. 3:26-cv-06345*, N.D. Cal., filed **25 Jun 2026**, Judge Noel Wise; plaintiffs' counsel Bathaee Dunne LLP; defendants Samsung, SK hynix, Micron. Sherman Act §1 theory: coordinated shift of wafer capacity to HBM to restrict conventional DRAM supply; alleges ~700% price rise over four years; seeks injunctive relief and treble damages ([TrendForce, 29 Jun 2026](https://www.trendforce.com/news/2026/06/29/news-samsung-sk-hynix-micron-face-u-s-class-action-lawsuit-over-alleged-dram-supply-manipulation/); [Quartz](https://qz.com/samsung-sk-hynix-micron-antitrust-class-action-memory-price-fixing-062526); [fnnews](https://en.fnnews.com/news/202607010955034256)).
- **Status:** no post-filing docket development found (motion to dismiss, consolidation). **Not obtained.**
- **Precedent, both ways:** SK hynix's predecessor Hynix pleaded guilty in the 2005 DOJ DRAM cartel case (USD 185m fine); but the 2018-vintage private DRAM class action was **dismissed in 2020 and affirmed by the Ninth Circuit in 2022** on the ground that parallel supply discipline without more does not plead a conspiracy. The current complaint's "capacity shifted to HBM" theory is structurally similar to the dismissed case. **My assessment: low probability of liability, non-zero cost of defence and disclosure; not a valuation driver unless DOJ opens a parallel criminal inquiry, of which there is no sign.**
