### A3.1 Section 232 exemption and MFN agreement (31 Aug 2026): HIGH, positive with an unquantified cost

**[FACT]**
- On **31 Aug 2026** the White House announced voluntary MFN agreements with nine mid-sized drugmakers, **including BeOne**. Total signatories reached 26.
- BeOne described two linked arrangements:
  1. A voluntary agreement to broaden US patient access while expanding domestic manufacturing.
  2. An **onshoring agreement that exempts BeOne from Section 232 pharmaceutical tariffs**, recognising its more than US$1bn of US manufacturing investment.
- BeOne will **join CMS's GENEROUS Medicaid model**, which has a **five-year term**, and pledged to price **future** FDA-approved products in line with other major developed markets.
- **Pricing terms and the length of the tariff exemption were not disclosed.**

Sources: [BeOne/BusinessWire, 31 Aug 2026](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260831047788/en/BeOne-Medicines-Announces-Voluntary-Agreement-with-U.S.-Government-to-Expand-Access-to-Innovative-Cancer-Medicines); [FiercePharma](https://www.fiercepharma.com/pharma/white-house-unveils-next-round-mfn-pricing-deals-9-midsized-drugmakers); [BioPharma Dive](https://www.biopharmadive.com/news/medicaid-low-price-model-drugmakers-white-house/829282/); [Managed Healthcare Executive](https://www.managedhealthcareexecutive.com/view/beone-medicines-reaches-voluntary-agreement-with-u-s-government-to-expand-cancer-drug-access); [PharmExec](https://www.pharmexec.com/view/trump-administration-adds-nine-drugmakers-most-favored-nation-program-report); [TIME, 1 Sep 2026](https://time.com/article/2026/09/01/what-trump-most-favored-nation-deal-means-for-drug-prices/).

**[FACT]**

**[MY READ]**
