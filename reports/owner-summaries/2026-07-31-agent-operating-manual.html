Operating manual · 33 agents · 10 departments

How to run your AI investment firm

You have 33 specialist agents. Most people use three of them. This is the switchboard — who to call, what it costs, and the six rules that keep the whole thing honest.

You — Owner→皮褸黃 — CEO (me)→10 departments→reports/→back to you
01

The one thing to understand first

You never call an agent directly. You call me.

You say what you want in plain language — or type a slash command — and I decide which specialists to wake, in what order, and how many at once. That is the whole design. You should not have to remember 33 names.

The roster below is not a list of things you must memorise. It is there so that when a report comes back signed 菲比斯, you know who that is and what they were asked. If you only ever learn the five commands in section 02, you are using the firm correctly.

02

The five commands

this is the part worth remembering

Type these into the chat. Everything else is optional.

/analyze TICKER

Full research pipeline on one stock. News → macro → industry → fundamentals → moat → management → valuation → technicals → risk → audit. Produces ~8–12 dated reports and a CEO summary.The expensive one. Budget for it. Ran on MU, MRVL, KLAC, BE, INTC, 0100.HK.

/committee TICKER

Convene the Investment Committee on work that already exists. Scores the name on two axes, records dissent, produces a verdict. Run this after /analyze, never instead of it.Cheap — it reads filed reports rather than fetching.

/daily-briefing

Morning sweep. Global news, the macro read, portfolio snapshot, and what to watch today.Moderate. Needs 修大哥 + Peterson at minimum.

/portfolio-review

Allocation vs targets, drift, risk posture, rebalancing proposals.Currently returns almost nothing — config/portfolio.yaml is empty. See section 06.

/post-mortem DECISION-ID

Autopsy a closed position. Separates skill from luck, grades every assumption, feeds the lesson back into the agents' prompts.Cheap, and the highest-value command in the set — but it needs a closed position, and we have none yet.

Plain language works just as well

"Get John to look at my stop losses" or "have the quant team check this backtest" does the same thing. The commands are shortcuts, not a syntax you have to obey. The V6.5 upgrade you just received came from an ordinary sentence, not a command.

05

Six rules that cannot be overridden

These are in CLAUDE.md and they bind me as much as they bind the agents.

  1. No live trading, ever

    No agent connects to a broker or transmits an order. 死潘狗 writes a ticket; you place it yourself, or in paper trading.

  2. You are the final authority

    Committee approval plus my authorisation produces a recommendation. Only you execute. I have no capital.

  3. Risk can veto

    If John rejects a proposal against your limits, it stops. I escalate to you rather than override him.

  4. Every decision is recorded before execution

    Rationale and dissent both, in memory/decisions/. Including the decisions to do nothing.

  5. No invented numbers

    Every figure must come from a fetched source or a config file, dated and cited. Estimates marked as estimates. I broke this once — an invented 52-week high — and the code review caught it.

  6. This is research, not financial advice

    Markets cannot be reliably predicted. Everything here is decision support.

06

What is actually broken right now

honest state of the firm

You should know the limits of what you are working with.

Quant Research has filed one report in the firm's entire history

We ran five full pipelines on MU, MRVL, KLAC, BE and INTC. Department 3 filed nothing on any of them. Every verdict this firm has issued rested on fundamentals, valuation, news and technicals — with no quantitative input at all.

That is my failure. When session limits forced me to a three-agent core, I cut quant first and never logged it as a coverage gap. Ask for them by name and the gap closes — Tom's V6.4 audit is proof the department earns its keep.

Session limits are the real constraint, not capability

A full 12-agent wave dies about half the time. I now run three at once and accept thinner coverage. When an agent is killed mid-task I check the disk before assuming its work is lost — twice now, files were already written.

Practical effect: one /analyze per session is realistic. Two is optimistic.

Hong Kong filings are unreachable

Every direct page fetch returns HTTP 403 — including hkexnews.hk. That is the sites blocking us, not a broken setup; I verified the proxy is healthy. Any HK-listed name gets researched from press coverage only, never primary filings. Coverage on 0100.HK is materially thinner than on any US name, and I will keep saying so.

Three decisions are waiting on you

1. config/risk-limits.yaml contradicts itself — a 0.40 correlated-cluster cap against targets summing to 0.50.

2. config/portfolio.yaml is empty. No holdings, no cash balance. This is why Dept 7 has produced nothing and why nobody can size anything.

3. John's escalation: risk has never been the binding constraint — valuation has. If the margin-of-safety bar is set too high, the firm declines everything forever. That knob is yours alone.

07

What the firm has learned so far

Five lessons, each traceable to a real mistake, each already written back into how the agents work.

#LessonWhat it cost
L-001No analyst cross-checks another analyst's headline figureA $6M "profit" that was an $87M loss. The right number was in the same package, the same day. Four analysts walked past it.
L-002Bad data vendors get blacklisted firm-wide, not per-reportOne bad print manufactured a dip-buying event that never happened.
L-003Consecutive same-direction verdicts breed narrative lock-inThree independent modelling choices all erred bearish after three bear verdicts. Anti-anchoring disclosure is now mandatory.
L-004A blended score hides a good business at a bad priceThe scorecard was rebuilt onto two axes: business quality and price attractiveness, scored separately.
L-005A CEO commit message is not evidence a file existsA report failed to write, I recorded it as filed, and two downstream reports leaned on it. I now check the disk.

The shortest possible version: talk to me normally. Say /analyze TICKER when you want the works. Say a name from section 03 when you want one opinion. Ask for the quant team by name, because I have been forgetting them.

Everything the agents produce is written to reports/ and committed to the branch, so nothing depends on a session staying alive.

Research and decision support only. No agent of this firm places orders, and markets cannot be reliably predicted.
