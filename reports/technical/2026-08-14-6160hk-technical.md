- **ONC is the price-setting venue and it trades after HK closes.** HK closes 16:00 HKT

**Operational consequence for the Owner and for 死潘狗:** for this name, the HK open is
substantially predictable from the prior ONC close. Anyone adding to or trimming this
position should look at ONC's US close *first* and treat the HK session as the
