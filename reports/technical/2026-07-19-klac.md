## 4. Relative strength vs SMH / SOX — and vs MU / MRVL (mid-July selloff)

The question: was KLA more resilient than the memory/AI-accelerator names? **Answer: yes on single-session shock, but nuanced peak-to-current.**

- **Single-session shock resilience — KLA outperformed.** In the worst sessions KLAC fell **~4%**, vs **MU ~-13%** (a single-day ~$138B wipeout; memory names dragged into a bear market with Samsung/SK Hynix) and **MRVL ~-5%**. KLA's process-control/WFE mix (diversified across logic, foundry and memory capex; not levered to memory *pricing*) cushioned it. **KLA is fundamentally the more defensive semicap holding.**
- **Peak-to-current — KLA underperformed the index.** SMH/SOX are down ~7–10% in the selloff (SMH ~-17% month-to-date); KLAC is down ~-31% from its high. KLAC had run the *most* into its split (near-parabolic), so it had the most to give back. **Resilient business, over-extended stock.**

**RS verdict:** KLA screens as the higher-quality, more resilient franchise than MU/MRVL — but its chart was the most stretched, so respect the deeper mechanical retracement. This supports a *buy-the-correction* posture for a fundamentally-justified name, executed in zones rather than at market.
