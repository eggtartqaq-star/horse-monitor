## Flow verdict: DISTRIBUTION — moderate confidence (~60%)

Price fell ~35% from the June 8 close of $288.85 to a July 17 close of $188.68 (intraday low $177.95), with the sharpest down days on above-average volume and short interest rising into the decline. Q1 13F data shows net institutional accumulation, but that is as of 2026-03-31 — it pre-dates the entire June peak and July selloff and cannot be read as current sponsorship. Confidence capped at moderate because per-print block/dark-pool data and per-strike OI were not accessible.
