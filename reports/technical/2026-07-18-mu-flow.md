## Flow verdict
**DISTRIBUTION — moderately high confidence (~70%).** Price is down roughly 30% from the June 25 all-time high ($1,213.37 close) to the ~$806–853 area, and the decline is occurring on *expanding* volume while up-days/bounces are on lighter tape. Insider selling at a 16-year high, a disclosed high-profile short, and a large disclosed institutional put book all corroborate supply. The offset: 13F data (as of 2026-03-31, so badly lagged relative to this move) showed one of the heaviest institutional accumulation waves on record, and options order flow is mixed rather than one-way bearish.

## Volume character
- **Blow-off then heavy-volume break.** Anthropic strategic agreement announced June 22 (HBM supply + Series H investment) drove the stock to records; record FQ3 earnings June 24 (revenue $41.46B vs $35.69B est., EPS $25.11 vs $20.49) sent shares +15–17% to the June 25 ATH, followed immediately by a -6% "wild week" reversal June 26 — a climactic, two-sided tape at the top.
- **Down-days on expanding volume:** July 15 -8% to -9% with roughly $49.2B in dollar volume traded (~50M+ shares vs. reported average daily volume of ~31.8M–43.5M shares — clearly above average). July 16 -5.65% ($904.28 → $853.20; some sources cite -8% intraday). July 17 continued lower into the $806–850 range. Successive wide-range down days on above-average volume is textbook distribution, not quiet profit-taking.
- Catalysts for the supply were sector-wide (ASML, then TSMC's capex reset to $60–64B, SK Hynix -15% and a KOSPI trading halt, CXMT's reported $8.55B IPO, CoreWeave exploring memory-price hedges) — so some of this is sector de-risking rather than MU-specific liquidation. That caps confidence below "high."

## Options flow read
- **Mixed, with put pressure building.** As of July 16: ~500 unusual option trades totaling ~$663.7M premium, split 59.6% call orders / 40.4% put orders, sentiment ~53% bullish / 47% bearish — order count leans bullish but conviction is split.
- **Put/call volume ratio ~1.00** (≈359,830 puts vs 361,469 calls) — elevated for a name that traded call-heavy through the AI melt-up; 90-day put/call OI ratio was 1.44 as of 2026-06-18. Skew has shifted toward downside protection.
- **Stale gamma map:** post-earnings positioning was anchored by heavy call OI at $1,300 and put protection at $1,100 (a ±15% envelope when the stock was ~$1,230 premarket). With spot now ~$806–853, both anchors are far out of the money — dealer gamma at those strikes is largely dead, which *reduces* pinning and leaves the tape freer to move. Current max-pain/heavy-OI strikes near spot could not be verified (data access blocked; see limitations).
- **Anecdote, not pattern, but notable:** Michael Burry disclosed a short initiated near $1,051.87 on July 1, stating puts "seemed expensive" (i.e., IV was rich) so he shorted stock and would add puts if vol eases. One print/position is an anecdote; flagged because it aligns with the observed skew shift.

Cannot confirm or deny large off-exchange accumulation into this decline — treat the block-trade picture as unknown.
