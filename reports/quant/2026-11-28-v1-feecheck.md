## 4. What would make this fail live

1. **Spread and slippage above 13 bp per side** wipes out the in-sample edge. Opening-auction limit fills could plausibly cost that much; it has not been measured.
2. **An edge that exists only from 2025.**
   - In-sample EV is +0.06% (Lab) and −0.37% (HK-2).
   - E2 loses money on every basis.
   - A regime like 2023–24 would mean trailing T-bills.
3. **The 2026 hindsight universe.**
4. **The stop is DAY, not GTC** (evidence §3.3). Missed daily re-placement is not modelled. That is for the CRO to rule on.
5. **The promotion ends.** The pricing headline shows no end date.
