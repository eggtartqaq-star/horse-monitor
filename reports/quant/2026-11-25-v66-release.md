  - The scanner's ① and the bot hide share counts and limit prices.

## Addendum: V6.6.6 (2026-11-26), lab only

**Why.** The Owner asked whether the system can run long-term as extra income. Answering honestly needs the lab to model two things it did not: the live entry, and interest on idle cash.

**What changed** (`cf41d84`, fingerprint `943960c97a9b`):
- `lab.py` gains four new options. Defaults keep the old behaviour, so `results/baseline.json` is identical.
  - `entry_rule="limit"`, the live rule (C-8 amended, N-15, N-26):
    - the share count and the maximum limit price are computed at the signal close;
    - next day, an open above the limit means no fill;
    - an open at or below the system stop means no buy;
    - otherwise the order fills at the open.
  - `capital_hkd`: capital, separate from the sizing base (CRO C-7: 12,500 / 10,000).
  - `cash_yield` and `cash_reserve_pct`: interest on cash above a reserve.
- **Unchanged:** scanner, ledger, bot, sizing, signals and guardrails.
- **Tests:**
  - selftest 117 → **126**, also under `-O`;
  - 4 mutants (limit test, floor, cost basis, reserve base), each caught;
  - equivalence 0 / 85,120;
