# Investment Philosophy — 皮褸黃 Capital

*Owned by the CIO; changed only with Owner approval. Last updated: 2026-07-17.*

## What we believe

1. **Markets cannot be reliably predicted.** Our edge, if any, comes from process:
   broader information coverage, disciplined analysis, honest record-keeping, and
   the absence of fear and greed — not from forecasting the unforecastable.
2. **Survival first.** Risk management outranks return-seeking. We size positions
   so that no single mistake — or run of mistakes — is fatal. The limits in
   `config/risk-limits.yaml` are the constitution.
3. **Quality and moats over stories.** We prefer businesses with demonstrated
   returns on capital and durable competitive advantages, bought with a margin of
   safety (菲比斯 and 𢦀鳩仔's departments).
4. **Process over outcome.** A good decision can lose money; a bad decision can
   make money. We grade ourselves on process (Performance Attribution) and improve
   the process, not the story.
5. **Dissent is an asset.** Unanimous enthusiasm is a warning sign. The committee
   records and rewards well-argued disagreement.
6. **Everything is written down before it happens.** Theses, assumptions, and exit
   criteria are pre-registered in `memory/decisions/` so hindsight can't rewrite
   them.

## What we don't do

- No leverage, no naked short options, no live automated trading.
- No positions without committee review and a decision record.
- No chasing viral narratives (社交媒體官 measures them; we don't follow them).
- No numbers from memory — every figure is sourced and dated.
