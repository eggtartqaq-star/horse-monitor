## Scores — TWO SEPARATE AXES (never blend them)

> **Why two axes** (instituted 2026-07-28 after the AI Auditor's B-1 finding on the
> INTC package): the old single-score format put ~60% of its weight on one variable —
> price versus fair value, including a binary margin-of-safety gate. The effect was
> that any stock trading near or above fair value was structurally capped around 40,
> regardless of how good the business was. Across five consecutive names it produced
> an 8–33 band and let the desk score its *best-improving* business (INTC) at 12,
> below two weaker ones. That is a false institutional record: it says the firm
> examined five bad businesses when it examined at least one good business at a price
> it did not like. **A great company at a bad price and a bad company at a fair price
> are opposite errors and must never share a number.**
